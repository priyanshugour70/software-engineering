## 26 – Checklists for Feasibility Studies

These checklists help ensure **thorough and focused Feasibility work**.

We include:

- A. At Feasibility start
- B. Before finalizing Feasibility
- C. Before handover to design/delivery

---

### A. At Feasibility Start – Checklist

- [ ] Feasibility charter defined (scope, timebox, team, deliverables).
- [ ] Problem statements and business case reviewed.
- [ ] Key feasibility questions identified (tech/ops/security/legal/etc.).
- [ ] Relevant stakeholders (arch, ops, security, legal, risk) identified and invited.
- [ ] Current architecture and process docs located.

---

### B. Before Finalizing Feasibility – Checklist

**Coverage**

- [ ] Technical feasibility assessed (architectures, integrations, data, NFRs).
- [ ] Operational feasibility assessed (processes, roles, SLAs, support).
- [ ] Security & privacy feasibility assessed.
- [ ] Legal & regulatory feasibility assessed.
- [ ] Organizational/change feasibility considered.

**Options & Trade-Offs**

- [ ] At least 1–2 solution options considered and compared.
- [ ] Preferred option clearly recommended with rationale.

**Risks & Constraints**

- [ ] Risk register updated with major risks across dimensions.
- [ ] Constraint log updated with hard and soft constraints.
- [ ] Owners and mitigation ideas assigned.

**Documentation**

- [ ] Feasibility summary document drafted.
- [ ] Option assessment summarized.
- [ ] References to supporting diagrams and notes included.

---

### C. Before Handover to Design/Delivery – Checklist

- [ ] Architecture/design teams agree on:
  - preferred option,
  - assumptions and open issues.
- [ ] Ops/support teams:
  - understand process and support implications.
- [ ] Security/legal/risk:
  - signaled major concerns and required controls.
- [ ] Governance bodies:
  - informed of key risks and constraints,
  - understand recommended path.
- [ ] Next steps and owners defined:
  - enabler work,
  - design activities,
  - further assessments (if needed).
