## 24 – Checklists for Business Case & ROI

These checklists help ensure **completeness and quality** of business case work.

We include:

- A. Before modeling and estimation
- B. Before presenting the business case
- C. After decision and post‑launch

---

### A. Before Modeling & Estimation – Checklist

**Inputs & Context**

- [ ] Clear problem statement(s) and scope.
- [ ] Research insights reviewed and integrated.
- [ ] Strategic goals and constraints identified.

**Drivers & Data**

- [ ] Value drivers listed (revenue, cost, risk).
- [ ] Cost drivers listed (build, run, change, vendors).
- [ ] Baseline data collected or requested (volumes, conversion, unit economics).

**Assumptions**

- [ ] Key assumptions written down with:
  - [ ] sources,
  - [ ] rationale,
  - [ ] confidence levels.
- [ ] Plan for conservative/base/aggressive scenarios.

---

### B. Before Presenting the Business Case – Checklist

**Model Quality**

- [ ] Build vs run costs clearly separated.
- [ ] Benefit calculations traceable back to drivers.
- [ ] At least three scenarios (conservative/base/aggressive).
- [ ] Sensitivity analysis for top 2–3 drivers.

**Narrative & Artifacts**

- [ ] Executive summary written in plain language.
- [ ] Clear tables/graphs for:
  - [ ] key metrics (ROI, NPV/payback),
  - [ ] scenarios.
- [ ] Risks and dependencies clearly listed, with mitigations.
- [ ] Recommendation stated (proceed/adjust/defer/stop, with conditions).

**Stakeholder Alignment**

- [ ] Finance partner has reviewed and validated the model.
- [ ] Data/BI has validated data sources and calculations where relevant.
- [ ] Engineering/ops have reviewed cost and feasibility assumptions.
- [ ] Risk/compliance consulted for regulatory/risk impacts.

---

### C. After Decision & Post-Launch – Checklist

**Decision Record**

- [ ] Decision outcome documented (approve/adjust/reject).
- [ ] Funding envelope and phasing recorded.
- [ ] Conditions or stage‑gates captured (metrics, timelines).

**Tracking Setup**

- [ ] KPIs and financial metrics defined for tracking.
- [ ] Analytics and reporting wiring planned or implemented.
- [ ] Owners assigned for benefit realization and monitoring.

**Learning & Updates**

- [ ] Plan for post‑implementation review scheduled.
- [ ] Mechanism defined to update business case with:
  - [ ] pilot results,
  - [ ] major scope or context changes.
