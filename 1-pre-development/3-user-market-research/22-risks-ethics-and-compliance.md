## 22 – Risks, Ethics & Compliance in User & Market Research

This file covers **ethical and regulatory considerations** that are especially important in banking and enterprises.

We cover:

- A. Ethical principles
- B. Privacy & data protection
- C. Regulatory and organizational risks
- D. Practical guidelines

---

### A. Ethical Principles

1. **Respect for participants**
   - Treat people with dignity and courtesy.
   - Avoid:
     - shaming or pressuring,
     - manipulative questions.

2. **Informed consent**
   - Participants should know:
     - why they are being researched,
     - what will happen,
     - how data will be used,
     - their right to stop at any time.

3. **Do no harm**
   - Be cautious with:
     - sensitive topics (e.g., debt, financial distress),
     - vulnerable groups.
   - Avoid triggering distress without support or benefit.

4. **Honesty & transparency**
   - Avoid:
     - misleading participants about purpose,
     - promising things research cannot deliver.

---

### B. Privacy & Data Protection

1. **Personal & Financial Data**
   - In banking, you often deal with:
     - names,
     - contact details,
     - account or transaction information.
   - Treat all such data as:
     - highly confidential.

2. **Data Minimization**
   - Collect only what you genuinely need.
   - Anonymize when possible:
     - remove or mask identifiers in datasets and notes.

3. **Storage & Access**
   - Use:
     - authorized tools,
     - secure drives,
     - controlled access.
   - Avoid:
     - copying sensitive data to personal devices or unapproved tools.

4. **Recording & Transcription**
   - Get explicit consent for:
     - recording sessions.
   - Follow:
     - your organization’s policies on storage and retention.

---

### C. Regulatory & Organizational Risks (Banking Focus)

1. **Regulatory Non-Compliance**
   - Risk:
     - contacting customers without proper consent,
     - storing data in non‑approved locations,
     - sharing regulated data with third parties inappropriately.
   - Mitigation:
     - consult:
       - legal,
       - compliance,
       - information security,
     before designing and running research involving customers and their data.

2. **Reputational Risk**
   - Unprofessional or insensitive research can:
     - harm trust,
     - generate complaints,
     - attract negative attention.

3. **Internal Policy Breaches**
   - Organizations often have:
     - specific policies on:
       - contacting clients,
       - incentives,
       - storing data.
   - Violating them can:
     - lead to disciplinary consequences,
     - undermine support for research.

---

### D. Practical Guidelines

1. **Align with Legal & Compliance Early**
   - Share:
     - your research plan,
     - participant criteria,
     - data use plans,
   before recruiting or collecting data.

2. **Use Approved Tools**
   - Only use:
     - storage and recording tools that have been vetted.
   - Avoid sending:
     - sensitive info over unapproved channels.

3. **Standardize Consent**
   - Work with:
     - legal/compliance,
   to create:
     - standard consent scripts and forms.

4. **Anonymize in Synthesis**
   - In reports and artifacts:
     - remove names and identifying details,
     - use pseudonyms or generic labels (e.g., “SME Owner – Retail”).

5. **Be Careful with Incentives**
   - Ensure:
     - incentives are appropriate and fair,
     - do not pressure participants,
     - comply with internal rules and tax laws.

6. **Handle Vulnerable Participants Carefully**
   - If research may surface:
     - distress (e.g., financial hardship),
   consider:
     - whether to include such participants,
     - how to provide support if distress arises.
