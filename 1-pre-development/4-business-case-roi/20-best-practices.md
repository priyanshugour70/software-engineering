## 20 – Best Practices for Business Case & ROI

This file summarizes **practical best practices** that lead to trustworthy, useful business cases.

We group them into:

- A. Mindset
- B. Modeling & assumptions
- C. Collaboration & governance
- D. Follow‑through

---

### A. Mindset Best Practices

#### 1. Decision Support, Not Just Approval

- Treat business cases as tools to **improve decisions**, not as:
  - hurdles,
  - or sales pitches only.

Aim:

- transparency about:
  - upside,
  - downside,
  - uncertainties.

---

#### 2. “Honest Numbers” Culture

- Resist pressure to:
  - inflate benefits,
  - hide uncertainties.
- Document:
  - realistic ranges,
  - downside scenarios,
  - what would change your mind.

This builds **long‑term trust** in your cases.

---

### B. Modeling & Assumption Best Practices

#### 3. Start from Drivers, Not Targets

- Avoid starting with:
  - “We need a 20M NPV”.
- Instead:
  - identify drivers (adoption, conversion, unit economics),
  - build the case bottom‑up from them.

---

#### 4. Use Ranges & Scenarios

- rarely know exact:
  - adoption,
  - conversion,
  - costs.
- use:
  - base, conservative, and aggressive scenarios,
  - sensitivity analysis for key drivers.

---

#### 5. Separate Build vs Run Costs

- always distinguish:
  - one‑time costs (build),
  - ongoing costs (run).

Avoid:

- underestimating total cost of ownership.

---

#### 6. Align with Finance on Methods & Rates

- agree early on:
  - discount rate,
  - NPV/IRR conventions,
  - treatment of CapEx vs OpEx,
  - inflation assumptions if used.

This prevents:

- re‑work and disputes later.

---

### C. Collaboration & Governance Best Practices

#### 7. Co-Create with Key Functions

- involve:
  - business owner,
  - PM,
  - finance,
  - data/BI,
  - engineering,
  - risk/ops,
in modeling and narratives.

Reduces:

- blind spots,
- late surprises.

---

#### 8. Fit into Portfolio Perspective

- frame cases:
  - not just on absolute merits,
  - but relative to:
    - other initiatives,
    - capacity,
    - risk appetite.

Link:

- to portfolio overviews and investment themes.

---

### D. Follow-Through Best Practices

#### 9. Track Actuals vs Business Case

- after launch:
  - compare:
    - actual adoption, revenue, cost changes,
    - with business case assumptions.
- learn:
  - why differences occurred,
  - update future estimation patterns.

---

#### 10. Update Business Cases When Context Changes

- when:
  - major scope shifts,
  - regulatory or market changes,
  - new insights from pilots,
occur:
  - revisit the case,
  - adjust assumptions and recommendations.

---

#### 11. Close the Loop with Retrospectives

- periodically:
  - run retros on:
    - past investment decisions,
    - quality of business cases.
- ask:
  - “Where were we over/under‑optimistic?”
  - “What assumptions should we treat with more caution next time?”
