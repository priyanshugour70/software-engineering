## 11 – Roles and Positions in User & Market Research

This file explains **who is involved** in User & Market Research and how they collaborate.

We cover:

- A. Core research roles
- B. Supporting roles
- C. Example team setups
- D. RACI-style overview

---

### A. Core Research Roles

#### 1. UX / Design Researcher

Responsibilities:

- Lead planning and execution of **qualitative research**:
  - interviews,
  - contextual inquiry,
  - usability tests,
  - workshops.
- Partner on:
  - research plans,
  - methods selection,
  - synthesis and insight generation.

Skills:

- Moderation, interviewing, facilitation,
- qualitative analysis and modeling (personas, journeys).

---

#### 2. Product Manager (PM)

Responsibilities:

- Ensure research is **decision‑driven** and grounded in:
  - problems,
  - strategy,
  - business needs.
- Co‑own:
  - research questions and hypotheses,
  - prioritization of what to study.
- Translate findings into:
  - updated problem statements,
  - requirements,
  - roadmap changes.

Skills:

- framing and prioritization,
- stakeholder management,
- connecting research to strategy.

---

### B. Supporting Roles

#### 3. Data Analyst / Data Scientist

Responsibilities:

- Conduct:
  - analytics deep dives,
  - segmentation,
  - funnel and cohort analyses.
- Help interpret survey data and experiments.

Contribution:

- Brings **quantitative backbone** to insights.

---

#### 4. Product Designer / Service Designer

Responsibilities:

- Collaborate on:
  - research design,
  - observation of sessions,
  - synthesis workshops.
- Translate insights into:
  - design concepts,
  - flows,
  - prototypes.

Contribution:

- Ensures insights are reflected in **actual UX and service design**.

---

#### 5. Business Stakeholders

Examples:

- Business owners, segment leads, sales leaders.

Responsibilities:

- Provide:
  - strategic context,
  - commercial priorities,
  - access to customers.
- Interpret findings from:
  - business perspective,
  - P&L, revenue, risk.

---

#### 6. Operations & Support

Roles:

- Operations managers,
- branch staff,
- call center leads.

Responsibilities:

- Provide:
  - frontline perspective,
  - process realities,
  - frequent customer issues.

Contribution:

- Critical for understanding:
  - backstage pain points,
  - feasibility of changes.

---

#### 7. Legal, Risk & Compliance (especially in banking)

Responsibilities:

- Ensure research:
  - follows privacy and ethics rules,
  - respects communication and data handling regulations.
- Interpret findings where they intersect:
  - risk and regulatory constraints.

Contribution:

- Enables research with real customers **safely** in regulated environments.

---

### C. Example Team Setups

#### 1. Startup / Small Team

- PM or Founder:
  - leads research planning and often does the interviews.
- Designer:
  - co‑facilitates and designs prototypes.
- Engineer:
  - helps with instrumentation, analytics, feasibility.

Research is often:

- lean,
- embedded in regular product work.

---

#### 2. Product Team in Mid‑Size Org

Typical composition:

- 1 UX/Design Researcher (shared across squads),
- 1 PM,
- 1 Designer,
- shared Data Analyst.

They:

- run timeboxed research sprints,
- cover multiple problems per quarter.

---

#### 3. Large Enterprise / Bank

Pattern:

- **Central research team**:
  - with UX researchers and service designers,
  - serving multiple domains.
- **Embedded PMs & Designers** in product squads.
- **Shared analytics & BI** teams.

Research may require:

- approvals from:
  - risk/compliance,
  - legal,
  - customer engagement teams.

---

### D. RACI-Style Overview (Simplified)

RACI = Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed.

| Activity                                    | Researcher | PM | Data/BI | Designer | Business | Ops/Support | Risk/Legal |
|---------------------------------------------|-----------|----|---------|----------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Define research goals & questions           | R         | A  | C       | C        | C        | C           | C         |
| Design research plan                        | R         | A  | C       | C        | C        | C           | C         |
| Recruit participants                        | R         | C  | C       | C        | C        | C           | C         |
| Run qualitative sessions                    | R         | C  | I       | C        | I        | I           | I         |
| Run analytics / quantitative analysis       | C         | C  | R       | I        | I        | I           | I         |
| Synthesize findings & create models         | R         | C  | C       | C        | C        | C           | C         |
| Present readouts & implications             | R         | A  | C       | C        | C        | C           | C         |
| Integrate findings into roadmap/requirements| C         | A  | C       | C        | C        | C           | C         |

Adapt this to your organization’s structure.
