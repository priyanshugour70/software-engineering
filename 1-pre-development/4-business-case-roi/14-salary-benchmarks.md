## 14 – Salary Benchmarks (Conceptual View for Investment-Facing Roles)

This file offers a **conceptual view** (not numeric) of how taking on Business Case & ROI responsibilities tends to correlate with seniority and compensation.

We cover:

- A. Why investment responsibility matters
- B. Relative levels
- C. Factors that influence pay

---

### A. Why Investment Responsibility Matters

- Deciding **where money and capacity are allocated** has:
  - large impact on company performance,
  - high visibility with leadership.
- Roles trusted to:
  - shape and defend investment cases,
  - steward P&L and capital,
are often rewarded with:
  - higher levels,
  - larger variable compensation components.

---

### B. Relative Levels (Conceptual)

From lower to higher responsibility:

1. **Individual Contributor (IC) – Analyst / PM / Engineer**
   - supports:
     - parts of business cases,
     - cost or benefit estimates,
   but doesn’t own final recommendations.

2. **Mid-Level PM / Lead Analyst**
   - owns:
     - business cases for specific features or projects,
     - within a single domain.

3. **Senior PM / Business Manager / Strategy Manager**
   - responsible for:
     - multiple initiatives,
     - trade‑offs across a portfolio slice.

4. **Product Director / Head of Segment / Head of Strategy**
   - shapes:
     - investment agenda for an entire product line or segment,
     - engages in:
       - annual planning,
       - capital allocation.

5. **VP / C‑Level (CPO, CFO, BU Head)**
   - ultimate accountability for:
     - portfolio of investments,
     - financial performance and risk.

As you move up:

- the **importance of Business Case & ROI skills** typically increases,
- direct hands‑on modeling may decrease, but:
  - ability to challenge, interpret, and communicate cases becomes crucial.

---

### C. Factors Influencing Compensation

1. **Industry & Regulation**
   - In banking and financial services:
     - misallocated capital and unmanaged risks are costly,
     - so strong investment discipline is particularly valued.

2. **Scope & P&L Responsibility**
   - Roles owning:
     - larger revenue pools,
     - broader portfolios,
     - multi‑country initiatives,
   generally map to higher bands.

3. **Company Stage**
   - Startups:
     - may combine product, strategy, and investment decisions into a few roles,
     - often pay via a mix of salary and equity.
   - Enterprises:
     - have more specialized roles,
     - with clearer fixed + variable compensation structures.

4. **Location & Market**
   - Major financial and tech hubs:
     - often offer higher cash compensation,
     - sometimes with long‑term incentives.

---

### D. Using This Information as a Practitioner

If you want to **grow** into higher‑impact, better‑compensated roles:

- build:
  - ability to craft and defend business cases,
  - track record of:
    - accurate enough forecasts,
    - responsible adjustments when data changes.
- signal this by:
  - leading investment discussions,
  - showing how your work influenced capital allocation.
