## 15 – Contexts and Domains for User & Market Research

Research principles are universal, but **how you apply them** depends on the domain.

This file covers:

- A. Banking and financial services
- B. SaaS and software products
- C. E‑commerce and marketplaces
- D. Operations‑heavy and regulated domains

---

### A. Banking and Financial Services

Characteristics:

- Strong **regulation and risk** concerns.
- Sensitive **personal and financial data**.
- Multiple **stakeholders**:
  - customers,
  - relationship managers,
  - risk/compliance,
  - regulators.

Research focus:

- Trust, security, and **risk perception**.
- Journeys with:
  - identity verification,
  - credit decisions,
  - complex product portfolios.
- Operational workflows:
  - branch,
  - call center,
  - back‑office.

Considerations:

- Extra care with:
  - consent,
  - data handling,
  - incentives,
  - topic sensitivity (e.g., debt, non‑payment).
- More involvement from:
  - legal,
  - compliance,
  - risk.

---

### B. SaaS and Software Products

Characteristics:

- Frequent releases and experiments.
- Rich **in‑product analytics**.
- Often B2B with:
  - multiple user roles,
  - integration into existing toolchains.

Research focus:

- onboarding & activation,
- workflow integration across tools,
- feature discoverability and adoption.

Methods:

- mix of:
  - interviews,
  - usability tests,
  - analytics,
  - in‑product surveys.

---

### C. E‑Commerce and Marketplaces

Characteristics:

- Clear, measurable funnels:
  - browse → add to cart → checkout → delivery.
- Strong link between UX and revenue.

Research focus:

- barriers to purchase,
- perceived trust and risk (delivery, payment, returns),
- seller and buyer workflows in marketplaces.

Methods:

- behavioral analytics,
- A/B tests,
- usability tests,
- post‑purchase surveys,
- interviews with buyers and sellers.

---

### D. Operations-Heavy & Regulated Domains

Examples:

- telecoms,
- utilities,
- healthcare,
- government systems.

Characteristics:

- complex processes,
- high impact of errors or downtime,
- layered regulations.

Research focus:

- end‑to‑end **service journeys**:
  - from request to fulfillment and support.
- coordination between:
  - frontstage and backstage actors.
- constraints from:
  - legacy systems,
  - SLAs,
  - safety rules.

Methods:

- contextual inquiry and shadowing,
- stakeholder interviews,
- journey mapping and service blueprints,
- limited direct user research where populations are vulnerable.

---

### What Stays the Same

Across domains, you still:

- start from problems and decisions,
- choose methods based on questions,
- combine qualitative and quantitative evidence,
- treat research as **ethical, respectful, and rigorous**.
