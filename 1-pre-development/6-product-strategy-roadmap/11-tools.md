## 11 – Tools for Strategy & Roadmapping

This file lists and explains common **tools** used in product strategy and roadmap work, with examples for enterprises/banks.

It focuses on:

- documentation & collaboration,
- whiteboarding & mapping,
- analytics & data,
- ticketing & delivery tracking,
- portfolio & roadmap tools.

The goal is **not** to advertise specific vendors, but to explain:

- what categories of tools you need,
- how they support each phase.

---

### 1. Documentation & Collaboration

Purpose:

- capture:
  - vision,
  - strategy,
  - problem/opportunity definitions,
  - business cases,
  - feasibility and risk assessments,
  - roadmaps and decisions.

Common tools:

- `Confluence`
- `Notion`
- `Google Docs`
- `Microsoft Word` (online)

Good practices:

- use:
  - templates for strategies and roadmaps,
  - consistent page structure across products.
- link:
  - from roadmap tools (e.g., `Aha!`, `Productboard`) back to detailed docs.
- keep:
  - “source of truth” pages updated and clearly owned.

---

### 2. Whiteboarding & Mapping

Purpose:

- explore and align on:
  - user journeys,
  - service blueprints,
  - opportunity solution trees,
  - portfolio maps and dependencies.

Common tools:

- `Miro`
- `FigJam`
- `Mural`
- `Lucidchart`

Use cases:

- design workshops,
- strategy offsites,
- roadmap **co‑creation** sessions with multiple teams.

Tips:

- maintain:
  - “clean” versions of key diagrams,
  - and “messy” collaboration boards.
- link:
  - diagrams to doc pages in `Confluence`/`Notion`.

---

### 3. Analytics & Data Tools

Purpose:

- provide **evidence** for:
  - problem sizing,
  - impact estimates,
  - success metrics for initiatives.

Examples:

- Product analytics:
  - `Amplitude`, `Mixpanel`, `Heap`, `Pendo`.
- BI & reporting:
  - `Tableau`, `Power BI`, `Looker`, `Qlik`.
- Data platforms:
  - `Snowflake`, `BigQuery`, `Redshift`, `Hadoop`.

Connection to strategy & roadmaps:

- measure:
  - baseline metrics before initiatives,
  - change in metrics after releases.
- feed:
  - insights into next strategy and roadmap cycles,
  - e.g., features that under‑delivered vs over‑performed.

---

### 4. Customer & Support Tools

Purpose:

- understand:
  - customer pain points,
  - support tickets,
  - operational incidents,
  - which inform strategy and priorities.

Examples:

- Support platforms:
  - `Zendesk`, `ServiceNow`, `Freshdesk`.
- CRM:
  - `Salesforce`, `Microsoft Dynamics`.

How they help:

- provide:
  - qualitative and quantitative signals about problems,
  - volume and severity of issues.
- help:
  - validate which themes and initiatives are most urgent.

---

### 5. Research & Discovery Tools (Input to Strategy)

Purpose:

- conduct:
  - user interviews,
  - usability tests,
  - concept tests,
  - diary studies.

Examples:

- Research tools:
  - `UserTesting`, `Lookback`, `Dovetail`.
- Recording & transcription:
  - `Zoom`, `Microsoft Teams`, `Google Meet`, `Otter`.

Link to roadmapping:

- insights inform:
  - which opportunities are real,
  - which solutions resonate,
  - where to invest capacity.

---

### 6. Ticketing & Delivery Tracking

Purpose:

- track day‑to‑day work:
  - epics,
  - stories,
  - tasks,
  - bugs.

Common tools:

- `Jira`
- `Azure DevOps`
- `Trello`
- `Asana`

Relation to strategy & roadmaps:

- epics and features in these tools:
  - should map to roadmap items,
  - and to strategic themes/goals.
- use:
  - labels or custom fields for:
    - theme,
    - initiative,
    - OKR.

This creates a line of sight from:

- company strategy → roadmap → epics → daily work.

---

### 7. Portfolio & Roadmap Tools

Purpose:

- represent and manage:
  - multi‑level roadmaps,
  - portfolios,
  - capacity allocations,
  - dependencies.

Examples:

- `Aha!`
- `Productboard`
- `airfocus`
- `Jira Align`
- `Planview`
- `Clarity`

Capabilities:

- visualize:
  - initiatives and epics on timelines or now/next/later views,
  - across multiple products and teams.
- link:
  - roadmap items to:
    - documents,
    - Jira epics,
    - OKRs.
- manage:
  - capacity,
  - funding,
  - scenario analysis.

---

### 8. Choosing Tools in Enterprises/Banks

Constraints:

- security and compliance requirements,
- data residency,
- integration with existing systems,
- vendor risk assessments.

Approach:

- start from **needs**:
  - e.g., “we need a shared roadmap view across 10 teams,”
  - “we need to track value vs spend by theme.”
- evaluate:
  - if existing tools (e.g., `Jira`, `Confluence`, `Excel`) can cover needs,
  - if specialized tools add enough value to justify complexity.

Tip:

- sometimes:
  - a well‑structured `Confluence` space + `Jira` + `Tableau` is:
    - **good enough**,
    - if maintained with discipline.

---

### 9. Beginner Checklist

- [ ] Do we have:
  - [ ] a clear place where strategy & roadmap docs live (`Confluence`/`Notion`/Docs)?
  - [ ] shared whiteboards for journeys, maps, and portfolio views?
- [ ] Are roadmap items:
  - [ ] linked to delivery tickets (e.g., `Jira` epics)?
  - [ ] tagged with themes and objectives?
- [ ] Do we use analytics:
  - [ ] to measure impact of roadmap items?
  - [ ] to inform next strategy cycles?

If many answers are “no,” tools may be **fragmented**, making strategy and roadmap hard to execute.
