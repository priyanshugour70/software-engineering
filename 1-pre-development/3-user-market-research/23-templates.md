## 23 – Templates for User & Market Research

This file provides **markdown templates** you can copy for your own studies.

We include:

- A. Research plan
- B. Interview guide
- C. Usability test script (lightweight)
- D. Research insight summary

---

### B. Interview Guide Template (Semi‑Structured)

```markdown
# Interview Guide – <Study Name>

## 1. Intro (2–3 minutes)
- Thank participant, introduce yourself and purpose.
- Explain:
  - session length,
  - topics,
  - recording (if any),
  - confidentiality and data usage.
- Confirm consent to proceed.

## 2. Background (5–10 minutes)
- Role and responsibilities:
  - "Can you tell me about your role and typical day?"
- Context:
  - "How long have you been running this business / working in this role?"

## 3. Main Topic – Current Process / Behavior (15–25 minutes)
- "Tell me about the last time you <did X>."
- "Walk me through step by step – what happened first, next…?"
- "What tools or services did you use?"
- "What was easy? What was difficult or frustrating?"

## 4. Problem and Pain Points
- "What are the biggest challenges when you <do X>?"
- "Can you recall a particularly good or bad experience?"
- "What do you wish worked differently?"

## 5. Needs and Preferences
- "If you could wave a magic wand and change how this works, what would you change?"
- "What matters most to you when choosing a provider / tool?"

## 6. Wrap Up
- "Is there anything we didn’t ask that you think is important?"
- Thank participant, explain what happens next.
```

### C. Usability Test Script (Lightweight)

```markdown
# Usability Test Script – <Feature / Flow>

## 1. Intro
- Welcome and thanks.
- Explain:
  - purpose is to test the **design**, not the person,
  - it's fine to run into issues.
- Confirm:
  - recording and note‑taking.

## 2. Background
- "Have you done <related task> before?"
- "What tools do you currently use to do this?"

## 3. Tasks

Task 1:
- Scenario:
  - "Imagine that you want to <scenario>..."
- Instructions:
  - "Please show me how you would do that here."
- Observe:
  - time to complete,
  - errors,
  - comments.
- Ask:
  - "What are you thinking right now?"
  - "What, if anything, is confusing?"

Task 2:
- <Repeat pattern for additional tasks>

## 4. Overall Feedback
- "How did this experience compare with how you do this today?"
- "What did you like most? Least?"
- "What would you change first?"

## 5. Wrap Up
- Thank participant,
- share how feedback will be used,
- handle incentive if applicable.
```
