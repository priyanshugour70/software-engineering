## 13 – Job Market for Business Case & ROI Skills

This file explains how **Business Case & ROI** capabilities appear in real job roles and career paths.

We cover:

- A. Roles where these skills are central
- B. How job descriptions talk about them
- C. Career progression

---

### A. Roles Where Business Case & ROI Skills Are Central

#### 1. Product Manager / Senior Product Manager

Responsibilities often include:

- “Build data‑driven business cases for new initiatives.”
- “Define success metrics and estimate impact of roadmap items.”
- “Collaborate with finance and strategy to prioritize investments.”

Strong ability to:

- frame value,
- estimate impact,
- and communicate trade‑offs

is a key differentiator for senior PM roles.

---

#### 2. Business Owner / P&L Owner

Titles:

- Head of Segment, Business Line Manager, General Manager, Tribe Lead.

Responsibilities:

- own revenue, cost, and risk outcomes,
- regularly:
  - propose,
  - evaluate,
  - and prioritize investments.

Business case skills are **core to the job**.

---

#### 3. Strategy & Corporate Development

Titles:

- Strategy Manager, Corporate Development, M&A Analyst, Portfolio Manager.

Responsibilities:

- evaluate:
  - growth opportunities,
  - partnerships,
  - acquisitions,
  - large internal programs.
- produce:
  - investment memos,
  - board materials.

Here, Business Case & ROI skills are combined with:

- market analysis,
- competitive intelligence.

---

#### 4. Finance & FP&A Roles

Titles:

- FP&A Analyst, Finance Business Partner, Investment Controller.

Responsibilities:

- partner with business and product to:
  - assess investments,
  - track performance vs plan,
  - manage budgets.

---

### B. How Job Descriptions Mention These Skills

Look for phrases like:

- “Build quantitative models to estimate the impact of product initiatives.”
- “Develop business cases and present to senior leadership or investment committees.”
- “Apply driver‑based forecasting and scenario analysis.”
- “Balance customer value, technical feasibility, and financial returns.”

In banking/enterprise contexts:

- “Work closely with risk, compliance, and finance to evaluate the financial and risk impact of new propositions.”
- “Support portfolio and capital allocation decisions.”

---

### C. Career Progression

#### 1. PM / Product Track

- Junior PM:
  - focuses more on:
    - delivery,
    - smaller feature work.
- Mid‑level PM:
  - expected to:
    - estimate impact of roadmap items,
    - contribute to business cases.
- Senior PM / Product Lead:
  - leads:
    - multi‑initiative business cases,
    - portfolio trade‑offs.
- Director / Head of Product:
  - deeply involved in:
    - capital allocation,
    - board‑level investment discussions.

---

#### 2. Strategy & Business Track

- Analyst:
  - builds models,
  - supports senior colleagues on cases.
- Manager / Lead:
  - crafts narratives,
  - frames options and recommendations.
- Head of Strategy / Portfolio:
  - shapes overall investment agenda.

---

#### 3. Finance Track

- Analyst:
  - supports modeling and reporting,
  - validates assumptions.
- Senior Analyst / Manager:
  - partners closely with business lines,
  - co‑owns major business cases.
- Finance Business Partner / Controller:
  - key voice in:
    - go/no‑go decisions,
    - performance tracking.

---

### D. Showcasing These Skills in CVs & Interviews

Highlight:

- **Context**
  - “Built business case for SME digital onboarding initiative.”
- **Work**
  - “Identified value drivers and cost drivers; built ROI and payback models with finance.”
- **Outcome**
  - “Case supported funding approval of X and prioritized initiative against Y and Z.”
- **Learning**
  - “Updated business case post‑pilot based on real adoption and performance data.”

This shows:

- financial thinking,
- collaboration,
- and impact.
