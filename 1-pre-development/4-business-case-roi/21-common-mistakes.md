## 21 – Common Mistakes in Business Case & ROI

This file lists frequent pitfalls and how to avoid them.

We group mistakes into:

- A. Modeling & assumption errors
- B. Collaboration and process errors
- C. Governance and follow‑through errors

---

### A. Modeling & Assumption Errors

#### 1. Single-Point Forecasts with No Ranges

Pattern:

- only one “precise” set of numbers,
  - no alternative scenarios.

Risk:

- hides uncertainty,
- overconfidence in fragile assumptions.

Fix:

- always include:
  - conservative/base/aggressive scenarios,
  - sensitivity analysis on key drivers.

---

#### 2. “Hockey-Stick” Revenue with No Driver Logic

Pattern:

- dramatic revenue growth curves,
  - unexplained by adoption, conversion, or price changes.

Fix:

- express revenue in:
  - driver terms (customers × usage × price × margin),
  - tie assumptions to:
    - data,
    - benchmarks,
    - research.

---

#### 3. Ignoring Run Costs

Pattern:

- focus only on:
  - build estimates,
  - ignoring ongoing:
    - infra,
    - licenses,
    - support,
    - compliance.

Fix:

- always:
  - separate build vs run,
  - include multi‑year run costs.

---

#### 4. Double-Counting Benefits

Pattern:

- counting the same benefit in multiple categories:
  - e.g., increased revenue and reduced churn derived from the same customers twice.

Fix:

- map benefits:
  - to distinct drivers,
  - reconcile with finance and data teams.

---

### B. Collaboration & Process Errors

#### 5. Building Cases in a Silo

Pattern:

- PM or business owner:
  - builds case alone,
  - brings it to finance/tech late.

Risk:

- unrealistic assumptions,
- late pushback,
- re‑work.

Fix:

- co‑create with:
  - finance,
  - data,
  - engineering,
  - ops,
  - risk,
  from early stages.

---

#### 6. Treating Business Case as One-Time Event

Pattern:

- creating a business case only at approval time,
  - never revisiting it.

Risk:

- disconnect between:
  - plan and reality,
  - no learning over time.

Fix:

- treat cases as **living models**:
  - update after pilots and major events.

---

#### 7. No Link to Actual Measurement

Pattern:

- business case defines:
  - metrics and targets,
  - but post‑launch tracking:
    - doesn’t match these definitions,
    - or doesn’t exist.

Fix:

- align:
  - case metrics with product/analytics KPIs,
  - ensure instrumentation exists.

---

### C. Governance & Follow-Through Errors

#### 8. Political Inflation of Numbers

Pattern:

- benefits inflated to “win” approval,
  - costs minimized politically.

Risk:

- misallocated capital,
- loss of trust in process,
- blame later when targets are missed.

Fix:

- promote:
  - “honest numbers” culture,
  - emphasize:
    - ranges,
    - uncertainty,
    - learning.

---

#### 9. Ignoring Non-Financial Constraints

Pattern:

- case looks good financially,
  - but ignores:
    - regulatory deadlines,
    - capacity constraints,
    - change saturation.

Fix:

- include:
  - capacity view,
  - regulatory context,
  - change impact in case and portfolio review.

---

#### 10. No Clear Decision or Ownership

Pattern:

- case is presented,
  - but outcome is vague:
    - “sounds interesting, let’s explore more”.

Risk:

- initiatives hang in limbo,
- unclear ownership of next steps.

Fix:

- ensure:
  - clear decision types (approve / reject / adjust / defer),
  - ownership for:
    - tracking,
    - revisiting later if deferred.
