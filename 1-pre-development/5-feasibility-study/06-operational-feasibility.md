## 06 – Operational Feasibility

This file explains how to assess **operational feasibility** – whether the organization can run and support the solution effectively.

We cover:

- A. Key questions
- B. Processes, roles, and SLAs
- C. Support, incidents, and monitoring
- D. Practical checklist

---

### A. Key Questions

- Can existing **operations and support teams** handle the new solution?
- What **process changes** are required (front‑office, back‑office, support)?
- Do we need **new roles, skills, or capacity**?
- Are our **SLAs and operating hours** compatible with the proposed solution?

---

### B. Processes, Roles & SLAs

1. **Process Mapping**
   - Map “to‑be” processes for:
     - onboarding,
     - fulfilment,
     - exceptions,
     - back‑office tasks.
   - Identify:
     - manual steps,
     - handoffs,
     - approvals.

2. **Roles & Responsibilities**
   - For each process step, clarify:
     - who is responsible,
     - what skills are required,
     - what tools they use.
   - Check:
     - whether new roles (e.g., specialist review teams) are needed.

3. **SLAs & Operating Hours**
   - Determine:
     - required response and resolution times,
     - coverage (24×7 vs business hours),
     - interaction with existing SLAs.
   - Identify:
     - gaps between current capabilities and target SLAs.

Outputs:

- draft **to‑be process flows**,
- impact assessment on:
  - teams,
  - SLAs,
  - capacity.

---

### C. Support, Incidents & Monitoring

1. **Support Model**
   - Who is the **first line of support** (frontline, call center, digital support)?
   - What is **second line** (specialists, engineering)?
   - How will:
     - escalation,
     - knowledge management,
     - training
   be handled?

2. **Incident & Problem Management**
   - How will incidents be:
     - detected,
     - triaged,
     - resolved,
     - communicated to users?
   - Are existing incident processes enough or do we need:
     - new runbooks,
     - new on‑call arrangements?

3. **Monitoring & Observability**
   - What metrics and logs are needed to:
     - monitor health,
     - detect anomalies,
     - measure SLAs?
   - Do we have:
     - enough coverage and dashboards,
     - alerting and on‑call practices?

Outputs:

- high‑level **support model design**,
- list of **monitoring and runbook requirements**.

---

### D. Practical Operational Feasibility Checklist

- [ ] To‑be processes mapped for key journeys (including exceptions).
- [ ] Impacts on front‑office, back‑office, and support roles identified.
- [ ] SLAs and operating hour requirements documented and compared to current capability.
- [ ] Need for new roles, skills, or training identified.
- [ ] Support model (L1/L2/L3) outlined.
- [ ] Incident and problem management considerations documented.
- [ ] Monitoring and observability needs captured.
- [ ] Operational risks and dependencies recorded in risk register.
