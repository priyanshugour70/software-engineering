## 09 – Data Sources for Business Case & ROI

This file lists **where to get data** for benefits, costs, and risk estimates.

We group sources into:

- A. Product & customer data
- B. Financial & operational data
- C. Risk & compliance data
- D. Market & external data

---

### A. Product & Customer Data

#### 1. Product Analytics

Sources:

- Amplitude, Mixpanel, GA, in‑house platforms.

Data:

- user volumes,
- funnel conversion rates,
- feature usage,
- retention and churn.

Use:

- estimate:
  - impact on volume, conversion, retention,
  - baseline behaviors for benefit models.

---

#### 2. CRM & Customer Master Data

Sources:

- Salesforce, Dynamics, internal CRM.

Data:

- number and types of customers (segments),
- product holdings and cross‑sell patterns,
- lifecycle stages and tenure.

Use:

- size:
  - eligible populations and segments.
- estimate:
  - potential adoption and cross‑sell opportunities.

---

### B. Financial & Operational Data

#### 1. Finance & Accounting Systems

Data:

- revenue and margin by product/segment,
- unit economics (e.g., profit per loan),
- cost allocations,
- cost of capital / hurdle rates.

Use:

- calibrate:
  - per‑unit revenue and profit,
  - discount rates for NPV/IRR.

---

#### 2. BI & Operational Dashboards

Data:

- process volumes and cycle times,
- SLA performance,
- cost‑to‑serve metrics,
- staffing and workload.

Use:

- estimate:
  - cost savings from automation and efficiency,
  - baseline operational performance.

---

### C. Risk & Compliance Data

#### 1. Risk Management Systems

Data:

- credit loss rates,
- fraud incidents and losses,
- operational risk events.

Use:

- estimate:
  - potential **risk reduction benefits**,
  - baseline risk exposure.

---

#### 2. Compliance & Audit Reports

Data:

- fines,
- remediation costs,
- control deficiencies.

Use:

- for initiatives:
  - driven by compliance or risk improvements,
  - inform baseline for avoided future costs.

---

### D. Market & External Data

#### 1. Market Sizing & Growth

Sources:

- industry reports,
- analyst research,
- regulator and central bank publications.

Use:

- contextualize:
  - growth assumptions,
  - adoption ceilings.

---

#### 2. Competitive Intelligence

Data:

- competitor pricing,
- feature sets,
- adoption stories in public reports.

Use:

- sanity‑check:
  - revenue projections,
  - pricing models,
  - time‑to‑market urgency.

---

### How to Use These Sources

- Combine:
  - product data (behavior),
  - financial data (value per unit),
  - risk data (loss rates),
  - market data (size and growth),
to build:

- defensible assumptions,
- clear driver‑based models.

Always:

- document:
  - which sources you used,
  - their dates,
  - known limitations.
