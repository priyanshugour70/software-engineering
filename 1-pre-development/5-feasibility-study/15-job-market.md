## 15 – Job Market for Feasibility & Architecture Skills

This file explains how **feasibility/architecture-oriented skills** appear in job roles and careers.

We cover:

- A. Roles where feasibility work is central
- B. How job descriptions describe this work
- C. Career progression

---

### A. Roles Where Feasibility Work Is Central

#### 1. Solution / Enterprise Architect

Responsibilities commonly include:

- “Assess feasibility and impact of proposed solutions.”
- “Define target architectures and transition paths.”
- “Collaborate with product, security, and operations to de‑risk delivery.”

Feasibility analysis is a **core part** of the role.

---

#### 2. Technical Lead / Engineering Manager

Responsibilities:

- estimate effort and complexity,
- identify technical constraints and dependencies,
- advise on technical options and risks.

Feasibility skills:

- help these roles:
  - give credible guidance,
  - influence scope and phasing.

---

#### 3. Platform / SRE / Infrastructure Leads

Responsibilities:

- evaluate:
  - performance,
  - scalability,
  - reliability,
for new initiatives.
- guide:
  - infrastructure and platform decisions.

Their input is crucial to **non‑functional** feasibility.

---

#### 4. Risk, Security, and Compliance Roles

Responsibilities:

- assess:
  - security threats,
  - regulatory constraints,
  - operational risk.
- determine:
  - whether and under what conditions solutions can be deployed.

---

### B. How Job Descriptions Mention Feasibility

Look for phrases like:

- “Evaluate technical and operational feasibility of new products.”
- “Assess impact of changes on existing systems and processes.”
- “Identify risks, constraints, and dependencies, and propose mitigations.”
- “Work with cross‑functional teams to ensure solutions are viable and compliant.”

In banking:

- “Ensure proposed solutions meet regulatory, security, and risk requirements.”
- “Participate in architecture and risk governance forums.”

---

### C. Career Progression

#### 1. Engineering / Architecture Track

- Senior Engineer → Tech Lead → Solution Architect → Principal / Enterprise Architect.
- Increasing focus on:
  - system‑level thinking,
  - cross‑team and cross‑domain feasibility.

#### 2. Product & Delivery Track

- PM → Senior PM / Program Manager → Portfolio Lead.
- Higher levels:
  - expected to understand feasibility trade‑offs,
  - frame options and risks for leadership.

#### 3. Risk / Security / Compliance Track

- Analyst → Specialist → Lead / Head of function.
- At senior levels:
  - shape the organization’s **risk appetite**,
  - define acceptable solution patterns.
