## 19 – Real-World Style Examples (Micro-Scenarios)

These short scenarios illustrate specific Business Case & ROI moves in practice.

We cover:

- A. Turning a vague “big number” into driver‑based logic
- B. Updating a business case after a pilot
- C. Comparing two initiatives in a portfolio view

---

### A. Turning a Vague “Big Number” into Drivers

Statement from stakeholder:

> “This should add at least 20M in SME revenue in three years.”

Problem:

- no explanation of:
  - where 20M comes from,
  - what must be true.

Business case move:

- ask:
  - “How many customers?”
  - “What products?”
  - “What adoption or conversion changes are we assuming?”

Breakdown:

- Suppose:
  - 50k target SMEs,
  - 30% adopt new lending journey,
  - each adopts an additional 1.5 products over 3 years,
  - profit per product/year = 400.

Estimated 3‑year incremental profit:

- 50k × 30% × 1.5 × 400 × 3 ≈ 27M.

Now:

- you have a **driver chain** you can:
  - challenge,
  - compare against data,
  - adjust in scenarios.

### B. Updating a Business Case After a Pilot

Initial case:

- predicted:
  - 50% adoption of new digital onboarding by Year 2,
  - leading to certain revenue and cost benefits.

Pilot results (6–9 months):

- adoption at:
  - 25% of target segment (half of assumption),
  - but:
    - better than expected completion rates,
    - stronger feedback from adopters.

Business case adjustment:

- update:
  - adoption curves,
  - conversion metrics,
  - cost assumptions (actuals from pilot).
- rerun:
  - ROI/NPV/payback.

Outcome:

- the case still looks positive but:
  - with longer payback,
  - lower NPV in base case.

Decision:

- proceed, but:
  - introduce:
    - additional adoption tactics,
    - more conservative assumptions for further expansions.

### C. Comparing Two Initiatives in a Portfolio

Two business cases:

1. **Digital SME Onboarding v2**
   - NPV (5 yrs): 8M,
   - payback: 3 years,
   - risk: medium,
   - strategic fit: high.
2. **New Consumer Rewards Program**
   - NPV (5 yrs): 10M,
   - payback: 4 years,
   - risk: high (depends on behavior change),
   - strategic fit: medium.

Portfolio view:

- limited capacity and capital,
- regulatory mandatory investments also in pipeline.

Decision move:

- may prioritize:
  - SME Onboarding v2 if:
    - strategic focus is B2B/SME growth,
    - risk appetite for consumer side is low.
- or:
  - choose both but:
    - phase them,
    - allocate smaller initial funding to the riskier program.

Lesson:

- Business cases are not evaluated purely on **headline NPV**;
- context, risk, and strategy matter.
