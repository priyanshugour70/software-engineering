## 12 – Data & Inputs for Feasibility Studies

This file lists key **inputs and data sources** needed to run a robust Feasibility Study.

We group them into:

- A. Business & product inputs
- B. Technical & architecture inputs
- C. Operational & risk inputs
- D. External & regulatory inputs

---

### A. Business & Product Inputs

- Problem statements and scope (`2-problem-definition/`).
- User & market research findings (`3-user-market-research/`):
  - user needs,
  - behaviors,
  - journeys.
- Business Case & ROI (`4-business-case-roi/`):
  - value drivers,
  - cost and risk assumptions,
  - targets.

Use:

- to ensure:
  - feasibility work focuses on **right problems and value**,
  - and not on arbitrary solutions.

---

### B. Technical & Architecture Inputs

- Current architecture diagrams:
  - system landscape,
  - integrations,
  - data flows.
- Platform and technology standards:
  - approved technologies,
  - reference architectures,
  - integration patterns.
- Capacity and performance data:
  - utilization,
  - incident history,
  - current KPIs.

Use:

- to assess:
  - fit to existing landscape,
  - where changes or new components are required.

---

### C. Operational & Risk Inputs

- Process documentation and SOPs:
  - current operating procedures,
  - SLAs and OLAs.
- Workload and staffing data:
  - ticket volumes,
  - manual processing metrics,
  - shift patterns.
- Risk and incident logs:
  - operational risk events,
  - known control issues,
  - past failures.

Use:

- to understand:
  - where operations may become a bottleneck,
  - current risk hotspots.

---

### D. External & Regulatory Inputs

- Applicable laws and regulations:
  - financial services regulations,
  - data protection,
  - consumer protection.
- Internal policies:
  - security,
  - risk management,
  - conduct and compliance guidelines.
- Vendor and partner documentation:
  - SLAs,
  - integration specs,
  - security whitepapers.

Use:

- to:
  - identify legal/regulatory constraints,
  - evaluate external dependencies and risks.
