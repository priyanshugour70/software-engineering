## 14 – Skills for Product Strategy & Roadmapping

This file explains the **skills** needed to perform Product Strategy & Roadmap work effectively.

It is useful for:

- PMs and leaders planning their growth,
- managers hiring or coaching people,
- beginners understanding what to learn.

---

### 1. Skill Categories Overview

Key categories:

1. **Product & Strategic Thinking**
2. **Customer & Market Understanding**
3. **Analytical & Financial Skills**
4. **Technical & Systems Understanding**
5. **Collaboration, Facilitation & Communication**
6. **Governance, Risk & Compliance Awareness**

We’ll go through each with examples.

---

### 2. Product & Strategic Thinking

Core abilities:

- define:
  - coherent product visions,
  - outcomes,
  - and strategies (see `04-vision-and-product-strategy.md`).
- make:
  - **choices and trade‑offs** (what not to do).
- think in:
  - multiple time horizons (H1/H2/H3).

Concrete skills:

- framing problems/opportunities:
  - clear problem statements,
  - linking to segment/company strategy.
- crafting strategies:
  - diagnosis,
  - guiding policies,
  - coherent actions.
- building roadmaps:
  - that connect strategy to execution,
  - at different levels (see `07-roadmap-levels-and-views.md`).

---

### 3. Customer & Market Understanding

Abilities:

- understand:
  - user needs and behaviors,
  - market structure and competition.
- connect earlier phases:
  - market opportunity discovery,
  - problem discovery,
  - user & market research,
  - into strategic decisions.

Concrete skills:

- interpreting research:
  - qualitative insights from interviews,
  - quantitative patterns from analytics.
- segmentation:
  - knowing which segments to focus on and why.
- positioning:
  - differentiating value proposition vs competitors.

Tools:

- familiarity with:
  - research tools (`UserTesting`, `Lookback`, `Dovetail`),
  - market intel sources (`CB Insights`, `Crunchbase`, `Similarweb`, `Owler`).

---

### 4. Analytical & Financial Skills

Abilities:

- reason about:
  - impact,
  - cost,
  - risk,
  - and value.
- interpret and build:
  - basic business cases and ROI models.

Concrete skills:

- working with metrics:
  - conversion, engagement, retention,
  - risk and loss metrics,
  - cost and efficiency metrics.
- prioritization modeling:
  - using RICE, WSJF, risk‑adjusted ROI (see `06-prioritization-frameworks.md`).
- financial basics:
  - understanding revenue, cost, margin,
  - reading P&L impact estimates for initiatives.

Tools:

- `Excel`, `Google Sheets`,
- BI tools (`Tableau`, `Power BI`, `Looker`),
- product analytics (`Amplitude`, `Mixpanel`, etc.).

---

### 5. Technical & Systems Understanding

You do **not** have to be an engineer, but you need:

- enough understanding to:
  - have productive conversations,
  - appreciate constraints,
  - and shape realistic roadmaps.

Concrete skills:

- basic architecture literacy:
  - front‑end, back‑end, APIs,
  - data flows and storage,
  - key platforms and their roles.
- understanding non‑functional aspects:
  - performance,
  - scalability,
  - resilience,
  - security and privacy (see `5-feasibility-study` module).
- systems thinking:
  - how changes in one component affect others,
  - recognizing cross‑team dependencies (see `10-dependencies-and-sequencing.md`).

Helpful practices:

- regularly join:
  - architecture reviews,
  - demos of platform work.

---

### 6. Collaboration, Facilitation & Communication

Abilities:

- bring diverse stakeholders together,
- create shared understanding and agreements.

Concrete skills:

- facilitation:
  - running workshops (vision, themes, roadmap),
  - time‑boxing, structuring discussion,
  - handling conflict constructively.
- storytelling:
  - explaining strategy and roadmap in:
    - simple terms for beginners,
    - concise terms for executives,
    - detailed terms for delivery teams.
- documentation:
  - writing clear strategy docs,
  - roadmap rationales,
  - decision and assumption logs.

Tools:

- collaborative docs (`Confluence`, `Notion`, `Google Docs`),
- whiteboards (`Miro`, `FigJam`),
- video tools (`Zoom`, `Teams`, `Meet`).

---

### 7. Governance, Risk & Compliance Awareness

Especially important in enterprises/banks.

Abilities:

- understand:
  - risk appetite,
  - regulatory constraints,
  - governance structures.
- bake these into:
  - strategy and roadmaps,
  - rather than adding them late.

Concrete skills:

- reading:
  - risk/compliance guidance and translating to product implications,
  - e.g., KYC/AML, PSD2, data privacy (GDPR).
- collaborating:
  - working with risk/compliance to design feasible solutions,
  - scheduling regulatory work properly in roadmaps.
- documenting:
  - risk and control considerations in strategy docs,
  - relevant sections in decision logs.

---

### 8. Growing These Skills (Learning Paths)

For PMs and product leaders:

- **Short term (0–6 months)**:
  - strengthen:
    - metrics and prioritization,
    - facilitation and communication,
    - basic financial understanding.
- **Medium term (6–18 months)**:
  - practice:
    - owning multi‑team roadmaps,
    - cross‑functional strategy workshops,
    - working with finance and risk partners.
- **Long term (18+ months)**:
  - grow:
    - portfolio/segment‑level thinking,
    - coaching others on strategy and roadmap,
    - influencing organizational structures and funding models.

Activities:

- shadow:
  - senior PMs or heads of product in strategy sessions.
- run:
  - small roadmap cycles end‑to‑end.
- study:
  - examples of good and bad strategies and roadmaps in your firm.

---

### 9. Beginner Checklist

- [ ] Can you:
  - [ ] explain your product vision and strategy in simple terms?
  - [ ] link roadmap items to themes and goals?
  - [ ] describe how success is measured?
- [ ] Do you:
  - [ ] understand the basic architecture and dependencies of your domain?
  - [ ] feel comfortable reading key metrics and simple ROI estimates?
- [ ] Are you:
  - [ ] able to run or co‑facilitate roadmap discussions?
  - [ ] involving risk/ops/finance appropriately?

If several answers are “no,” this file plus adjacent ones in this module give you a **learning roadmap for yourself**.
