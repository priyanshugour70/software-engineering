## 20 – Best Practices for User & Market Research

This file collects **practical best practices** to make research impactful and sustainable.

We group them into:

- A. Mindset
- B. Process
- C. Collaboration
- D. Documentation

---

### A. Mindset Best Practices

#### 1. Decision-First Research

- Always start with:
  - “What decision will this research help us make?”
  - “What would we do differently based on possible outcomes?”

This keeps research **focused and valuable**.

---

#### 2. Curiosity + Skepticism

- Be genuinely curious about users,
- but also:
  - challenge your own assumptions,
  - look for disconfirming evidence.

Avoid:

- running research just to **confirm** a predetermined solution.

---

#### 3. Embrace Mixed Methods

- Combine:
  - qualitative (why, how),
  - quantitative (how often, how many).

Triangulation:

- increases confidence,
- surfaces nuances single methods miss.

---

### B. Process Best Practices

#### 4. Timebox and Scope Studies

- Avoid endless research:
  - set time limits,
  - define specific questions,
  - aim for “good enough” for decisions.

---

#### 5. Start with What You Already Know

- Review:
  - analytics,
  - tickets,
  - prior research,
  - market reports,
before designing new studies.

Prevents:

- duplicated work,
- missing obvious existing insights.

---

#### 6. Recruit the Right Mix of Participants

- Define:
  - key segments,
  - behaviors,
  - roles.
- Include:
  - both:
    - heavy users,
    - light or non‑users,
when relevant.

---

### C. Collaboration Best Practices

#### 7. Involve the Whole Team

- Invite:
  - PMs,
  - designers,
  - engineers,
  - business stakeholders,
to observe sessions (when allowed).

Benefits:

- builds empathy,
- reduces second‑hand interpretation problems.

---

#### 8. Co-Synthesize

- Run synthesis sessions with:
  - research,
  - product,
  - design,
  - sometimes ops/support.

Benefits:

- brings:
  - diverse perspectives,
  - better buy‑in on insights.

---

#### 9. Share Early and Often

- Don’t wait for a “perfect” report.
- Share:
  - interim patterns,
  - early surprises,
  - partial findings,
while clearly labeling them as preliminary.

---

### D. Documentation Best Practices

#### 10. Separate Raw Data from Insights

- Raw data:
  - transcripts,
  - full notes,
  - survey responses,
  - analytics exports.
- Insights:
  - short, clear statements summarizing what the data means and why it matters.

Make sure:

- insights **link back** to raw data for verification.

---

#### 11. Make Artifacts Easy to Find & Reuse

- Use:
  - consistent naming,
  - tagging (segment, journey, date),
  - central repository.

Goal:

- anyone can:
  - discover prior research,
  - avoid re‑doing studies.

---

#### 12. Be Transparent About Limitations

- Always note:
  - sample size,
  - sampling biases,
  - method constraints,
  - what you did **not** cover.

This builds:

- trust in research,
- realistic expectations about how to use it.
