## 24 – Governance, Alignment & Cadence

This file describes **how to organize recurring forums and cadences** that support Product Strategy & Roadmap work.

It focuses on:

- alignment across stakeholders,
- decision‑making structures,
- practical rhythms (monthly, quarterly, yearly).

---

### 1. Why Governance Matters (Especially in Enterprises/Banks)

Without clear governance:

- decisions:
  - happen in ad‑hoc side conversations,
  - depend on who shouts loudest,
  - are not documented or remembered.

With good governance:

- there are:
  - predictable forums
  - where:
    - strategy,
    - priorities,
    - dependencies,
    - and risks
  - are discussed and decided.

Goal:

- **enable** good decisions and adaptation,
- not create bureaucracy for its own sake.

---

### 2. Key Types of Forums

Common forum types:

1. **Strategy & Theme Review**
2. **Portfolio & Roadmap Review**
3. **Cross‑Team Dependency & Risk Sync**
4. **Regulatory & Risk Governance Forums**
5. **Team‑Level Planning & Retro Cadences**

You do not need separate meetings for all of these; in smaller contexts, some can be combined.

---

### 3. Strategy & Theme Review (Annual / Semi‑Annual)

Purpose:

- set or refresh:
  - product vision,
  - strategic themes,
  - high‑level bets for the next 1–3 years.

Cadence:

- annually, with:
  - light updates mid‑year if needed.

Participants:

- product and business leadership,
- key engineering and architecture leaders,
- risk/compliance and finance as relevant.

Inputs:

- outcomes from past year(s),
- market and user research,
- business case and feasibility outputs,
- regulatory horizon scanning.

Outputs:

- confirmed or updated:
  - vision,
  - strategic themes,
  - major bets and constraints.

---

### 4. Portfolio & Roadmap Review (Quarterly)

Purpose:

- review:
  - progress on themes and key metrics,
  - status of major initiatives,
  - dependencies, risks, and capacity.
- adjust:
  - roadmap for next 1–3 quarters.

Cadence:

- every quarter.

Participants:

- heads of product/portfolio,
- product leads,
- engineering leadership,
- business owners,
- risk/compliance and operations reps.

Typical agenda:

1. Outcomes review vs objectives (see `05-strategic-themes-goals-and-okrs.md`).
2. Status of major initiatives and risks.
3. New/changed regulatory or market constraints.
4. Proposed roadmap adjustments:
   - items moved in/out,
   - capacity re‑allocation.
5. Decisions and next steps.

Outputs:

- updated roadmap views,
- explicit decisions logged (`12-documents-and-artifacts.md`),
- clear communication plan for changes.

---

### 5. Cross‑Team Dependency & Risk Sync (Monthly / Bi‑Weekly)

Purpose:

- manage:
  - cross‑team dependencies,
  - shared platform work,
  - key delivery risks.

Cadence:

- monthly or every 2 weeks, depending on complexity.

Participants:

- PMs and engineering leads from:
  - product teams,
  - platform teams,
  - risk/ops teams with shared work.

Agenda:

- review:
  - dependency maps (`10-dependencies-and-sequencing.md`),
  - current and upcoming integration work,
  - blockers and mitigations.

Outputs:

- updated dependency views,
- actions assigned to clear owners,
- potential roadmap adjustments flagged for portfolio review.

---

### 6. Regulatory & Risk Governance Forums

Purpose:

- ensure:
  - product strategies and roadmaps:
    - respect risk appetite,
    - meet regulatory expectations.

Forms:

- existing risk committees,
- product/risk joint forums,
- model risk or compliance boards.

Cadence:

- varies (monthly/quarterly),
- plus ad‑hoc when major changes proposed.

Integration with product governance:

- major roadmap items:
  - with significant risk/regulatory implications,
  - should route through these forums early.

Outputs:

- approvals, conditions, or redirects for:
  - sensitive initiatives,
  - regulatory change programs.

---

### 7. Team‑Level Planning & Retro Cadences

At squad/team level:

- keep:
  - standard agile cadences:
    - sprint or iteration planning,
    - reviews and demos,
    - retrospectives.

Connection to strategy & roadmap:

- ensure:
  - epics and stories:
    - are clearly linked to initiatives and themes,
  - teams:
    - can trace their work up to outcomes and strategy.

---

### 8. Practical Tips for Healthy Governance

- **Time‑box and focus**:
  - avoid turning every forum into a generic status meeting.
- **Use clear inputs and outputs**:
  - standard templates,
  - clear decisions and owners.
- **Limit forum sprawl**:
  - too many overlapping committees confuse people.
- **Document and share**:
  - notes,
  - decisions,
  - updated artifacts.
- **Review governance annually**:
  - simplify where possible,
  - keep only what adds value.

---

### 9. Beginner Checklist

- [ ] Do you have:
  - [ ] at least one regular forum where product strategy and themes are discussed?
  - [ ] a quarterly‑ish forum to review and adjust roadmaps?
  - [ ] a way to manage cross‑team dependencies and risks?
  - [ ] appropriate engagement with risk/compliance forums if regulated?
- [ ] Are decisions and changes:
  - [ ] documented,
  - [ ] communicated clearly?

If not, start by introducing a **lightweight quarterly roadmap review**, and build from there.
