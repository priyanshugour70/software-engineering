## 13 – Job Market for Market Opportunity Discovery Skills

This file explains how **market opportunity discovery skills show up in real job roles**, what titles to look for, and how these skills affect **career prospects and salaries** (high‑level, not country‑specific).

We cover:

- A. Roles that heavily use discovery skills
- B. How job descriptions talk about discovery
- C. Career paths and seniority levels

---

### A. Roles That Heavily Use Market Opportunity Discovery

Even if the job title does not say “discovery”, many roles rely on these skills.

#### 1. Product Manager / Senior Product Manager

Typical phrases in job ads:

- “Identify and validate new product opportunities.”
- “Conduct market and customer research to shape the roadmap.”
- “Own the product discovery and delivery lifecycle.”

Discovery skills used:

- Understanding customer needs.
- Translating strategy into **opportunity backlogs**.
- Evaluating and prioritizing initiatives.

---

#### 2. Product Director / Head of Product / VP Product

Focus shifts from **single products** to **portfolios and strategy**.

Discovery responsibilities:

- Set **discovery themes** and **investment directions**.
- Oversee cross‑team opportunity pipelines.
- Decide which opportunities become **funded bets**.

These roles require:

- Strong strategic thinking,
- Ability to synthesize across multiple markets and segments.

---

#### 3. Strategy & Corporate Development Roles

Titles:

- Strategy Manager, Corporate Strategy, Business Development Manager, Growth Strategy, Portfolio Manager.

Discovery技能 use:

- Analyze markets, competitors, and macro trends.
- Identify **new business lines, segments, and partnerships**.
- Work closely with product teams to turn **strategic themes** into concrete opportunities.

---

#### 4. Innovation / New Ventures Roles

Titles:

- Head of Innovation, Innovation Manager, Venture Builder, New Business Lead.

Discovery responsibilities:

- Explore **new markets or business models**.
- Run experiments and pilots in **emerging spaces** (e.g., open banking, embedded finance).
- Collaborate with startups, partners, or incubators.

Discovery skills are central; these roles often talk explicitly about:

- “opportunity scanning”,
- “ideation and validation”,
- “incubating new products”.

---

#### 5. UX Research & Service Design Roles

Titles:

- UX Researcher, Design Researcher, Service Designer.

Discovery responsibilities:

- Deeply understand **customers and journeys**.
- Co‑create and test **new concepts** with users.
- Partner with product and strategy to **inform opportunity decisions**.

These roles may not own the **final opportunity prioritization**, but they are critical in generating **insights that feed discovery**.

---

### B. How Job Descriptions Mention Discovery (What to Look For)

When reading job ads, look for words and phrases like:

- “Market analysis”, “opportunity assessment”, “whitespace analysis”.
- “Customer discovery”, “problem discovery”, “hypothesis‑driven”.
- “Define and prioritize opportunities based on data and insights.”
- “Partner with data, research, and strategy to identify new growth areas.”
- “Build business cases and recommend new initiatives.”

In banking/enterprise contexts:

- “Work with risk, compliance, and operations to assess feasibility of new opportunities.”
- “Identify impacts of new regulations on product and market strategy.”

These are signals that **market opportunity discovery** is a major part of the role.

---

### C. Career Paths and How Discovery Skills Help

#### 1. From Junior PM / Analyst to Senior PM

Strong discovery skills help you:

- Move from **“ticket taker”** (receiving requirements) to **“problem owner”** (shaping what should be built).
- Demonstrate:
  - strategic thinking,
  - ability to drive impact beyond single features.

Hiring managers often look for:

- Evidence that you **identified** and **validated** opportunities, not just shipped tasks.

---

#### 2. From Senior PM to Product Lead / Director

At higher levels, you are expected to:

- Manage **opportunity portfolios** across teams.
- Align opportunities with **company level strategy and P&L**.
- Influence **investment and resourcing decisions**.

Demonstrating:

- successful past bets (where discovery led to high‑impact products),
- ability to **kill or pivot** weak opportunities early,

is a strong differentiator for these roles.

---

#### 3. From Product to Strategy or Innovation Roles

Because market opportunity discovery sits at the intersection of:

- strategy,
- customer understanding,
- and execution,

it is an excellent foundation if you want to:

- move into **corporate strategy**,
- lead an **innovation lab or incubator**,
- become a **founder** or **entrepreneur in residence**.

---

### D. How to Demonstrate Discovery Skills in Your Career

When writing CVs or preparing for interviews, emphasize:

- **What opportunity** you identified:
  - “Identified a gap in SME onboarding where 60% of customers abandoned at KYC step.”
- **How you discovered it**:
  - “Combined funnel analytics, support tickets, and 10 customer interviews.”
- **What decision it led to**:
  - “Prioritized a digital KYC initiative that became the team’s top roadmap item.”
- **What impact it had** (if known):
  - “Reduced onboarding time by 40% and increased conversion by 15%.”

This tells a **full discovery story**: from problem, through analysis, to action and results.
