## 25 – Templates for Product Strategy & Roadmapping

This file provides **practical text templates and outlines** you can copy into your own tools (`Confluence`, `Notion`, `Docs`, etc.).

They are intentionally simple and can be adapted to your context.

---

### 1. Product Vision & Strategy Document Template

**Title:** Product Vision & Strategy – \<Product / Domain>

**1. Context & Diagnosis (1–2 pages)**

- Market and customer context:
  - key trends,
  - opportunities,
  - threats.
- Internal context:
  - current performance,
  - strengths and weaknesses,
  - major constraints (technology, regulation, operations).

**2. Vision (2–4 sentences)**

- \<Vision statement using a variant of:  
  “For \<who>, we provide \<what>, so they can \<value>, in a way that is \<differentiator>.”>

**3. Strategic Pillars / Themes**

- Theme 1 – \<Name>  
  Short description (why it matters, what it covers).
- Theme 2 – \<Name>  
  Short description.
- Theme 3 – \<Name>  
  Short description.

**4. Where We Will / Will Not Play**

- We will focus on:
  - \<segments>,
  - \<geographies>,
  - \<product types>,
  - \<channels>.
- We will **not** focus on (for this horizon):
  - \<segments>,
  - \<geographies>,
  - \<product types>,
  - \<channels>.

**5. How We Will Win / Be Valuable**

- Differentiators:
  - \<experience / speed / data / ecosystem, etc.>
- How this links to:
  - customer jobs / pains,
  - competitive positioning.

**6. Key Bets & Capabilities**

- Bet 1 – \<Name>:
  - Description,
  - Supported themes,
  - High‑level outcomes.
- Bet 2 – \<Name>:
  - Description,
  - Supported themes,
  - High‑level outcomes.

**7. Risks, Constraints & Assumptions**

- Key risks and mitigations,
- Major constraints,
- Assumptions that could invalidate the strategy.

### 2. Strategic Themes & Goals (OKR) Template

**Title:** Strategic Themes & Goals – \<Product / Domain>

**Theme 1 – \<Name>**

- Description:
  - \<1–3 sentences>.
- Objective:
  - \<qualitative outcome>.
- Key Results / Metrics:
  - KR1 – \<metric and target>,
  - KR2 – \<metric and target>,
  - KR3 – \<metric and target>.

**Theme 2 – \<Name>**

- Description, objective, KRs as above.

**Theme 3 – \<Name>**

- Description, objective, KRs as above.

Optional:

- Mapping to company/segment OKRs.

### 3. Initiative & Epic Catalogue Template

Can be a table in `Confluence`/`Notion`/`Sheets`.

**Columns:**

- ID
- Name
- Type (Initiative / Epic)
- Short Description / Problem Statement
- Themes (1–2)
- Linked Objectives / OKRs
- Owner Team / Squad
- Status (Idea / Discovery / Prioritized / In Progress / Done / On Hold)
- Value Score (RICE / WSJF / other)
- Effort / Size
- Dependencies
- Links (business case, discovery docs, designs)

Use this as the **single source of truth** for major work items.

---

### 4. Roadmap View – Now / Next / Later Template

Useful for team or product‑level communication.

**Now (0–3 months – committed/active)**

- Initiative / Epic A – \<short description, themes, outcomes>.
- Initiative / Epic B – \<...>.

**Next (3–9 months – likely)**

- Initiative / Epic C – \<short description>.
- Initiative / Epic D – \<...>.

**Later (9+ months – options / exploration)**  

- Initiative / Epic E – \<high‑level idea>.
- Initiative / Epic F – \<...>.

Add:

- legend for:
  - theme colors,
  - confidence levels (e.g., solid vs dashed).

### 5. Decision & Assumption Log Template

**Table columns:**

- Date
- Decision
- Alternatives Considered
- Rationale (including risks and assumptions)
- Decision Makers (names/roles)
- Impacted Artifacts (strategy doc, roadmap views, initiative IDs)
- Follow‑up Actions

Example row:

- Date:
  - 2026‑03‑10
- Decision:
  - Move “Operations Workbench” initiative from H1 to H2.
- Alternatives:
  - Keep as is and add temporary capacity,
  - Reduce scope of “Digital Application v2.”
- Rationale:
  - regulatory initiative XYZ must be brought forward,
  - platform team capacity is constrained.
- Decision Makers:
  - Head of SME Banking, Head of Product, Risk Lead, Tech Lead.
- Impact:
  - updated H1/H2 roadmap views,
  - communicated to operations leadership.

### 6. Quarterly Roadmap Review Template (Agenda)

**Meeting:** Quarterly Roadmap & Portfolio Review – \<Domain>  
**Duration:** 90–120 minutes

**1. Objectives of This Session (5 min)**

- Confirm or adjust:
  - priorities,
  - major bets,
  - capacity allocation for next 1–3 quarters.

**2. Outcomes vs Objectives (20–30 min)**

- Review key metrics per theme,
- Learnings from past quarter’s initiatives.

**3. Status of Major Initiatives (20–30 min)**

- Highlight:
  - progress,
  - key risks,
  - dependency issues.

**4. New Constraints / Opportunities (15–20 min)**

- Regulatory changes,
- Market shifts,
- Internal capacity changes.

**5. Proposed Roadmap Adjustments (20–30 min)**

- Items to add, move, or remove,
- Capacity re‑allocations.

**6. Decisions & Next Steps (10–15 min)**

- Explicit decisions,
- Owners,
- Communication plan.

---

### 7. Beginner Tips for Using Templates

- Start with:
  - the minimal set:
    - strategy doc,
    - themes & goals,
    - initiative catalogue,
    - simple roadmap view.
- Don’t aim for:
  - perfect formatting,
  - heavy slides;
  - focus on **clarity and completeness**.
- Iterate:
  - based on feedback from teams and stakeholders.
