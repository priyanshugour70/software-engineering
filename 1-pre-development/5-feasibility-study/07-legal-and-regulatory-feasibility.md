## 07 – Legal & Regulatory Feasibility

This file focuses on **whether a solution is allowed and compliant** under laws, regulations, and internal policies, with emphasis on banking.

We cover:

- A. Key questions
- B. Typical regulatory areas (banking focus)
- C. Practical assessment steps
- D. Checklist

---

### A. Key Questions

- Is the proposed solution **permitted** under:
  - external laws and regulations,
  - internal policies and standards?
- Does it:
  - require new licenses or approvals,
  - change existing regulatory reporting,
  - impact capital, liquidity, or risk frameworks?
- Are there:
  - **jurisdiction‑specific** constraints,
  - data residency requirements?

---

### B. Typical Regulatory Areas (Banking)

1. **KYC / AML / Sanctions**
   - identity verification requirements,
   - ongoing monitoring obligations,
   - sanctions screening.

2. **Lending & Credit Regulations**
   - rules on:
     - creditworthiness assessment,
     - disclosure,
     - interest and fees,
     - responsible lending.

3. **Payments & Money Transmission**
   - payment scheme rules,
   - PSD2 / open banking regulations (where applicable),
   - settlement and reconciliation requirements.

4. **Data Protection & Banking Secrecy**
   - data privacy laws (e.g., GDPR),
   - banking secrecy obligations,
   - cross‑border data transfers,
   - customer consent requirements.

5. **Consumer Protection**
   - marketing and disclosure rules,
   - fair treatment,
   - complaints handling.

---

### C. Practical Assessment Steps

1. **Engage Legal & Compliance Early**
   - share:
     - initiative summary,
     - high‑level solution options,
     - affected products and jurisdictions.

2. **Identify Applicable Regulations & Policies**
   - list key regulations per jurisdiction,
   - capture internal policies (e.g., risk, security, conduct).

3. **Map Requirements to Solution Elements**
   - for each key requirement, assess:
     - how current/proposed solution satisfies it,
     - where gaps or uncertainties exist.

4. **Capture Constraints, Gaps & Ambiguities**
   - hard constraints:
     - laws or rules that cannot be violated.
   - soft constraints:
     - areas where interpretation or approvals may vary.

5. **Agree on Mitigation and Approvals**
   - design:
     - process or technical controls,
     - additional disclosures,
     - manual checks where necessary.
   - define:
     - approvals needed (e.g., risk committee, legal sign‑off).

Outputs:

- a **legal/regulatory feasibility summary**,
- list of:
  - constraints,
  - required controls,
  - approvals and next steps.

---

### D. Legal & Regulatory Feasibility Checklist

- [ ] Legal/compliance engaged and briefed on initiative.
- [ ] Applicable regulations and internal policies identified by jurisdiction.
- [ ] Key regulatory requirements mapped to solution components.
- [ ] Data protection and cross‑border considerations assessed.
- [ ] Any licensing or regulatory notifications/approvals identified.
- [ ] Hard constraints and soft constraints documented.
- [ ] Required controls and mitigations described (e.g., additional checks, disclosures).
- [ ] Needed approvals (committees, sign‑offs) listed.
- [ ] Legal/regulatory risks recorded in risk register with owners.
