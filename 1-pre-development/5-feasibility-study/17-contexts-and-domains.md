## 17 – Contexts and Domains for Feasibility Studies

This file highlights how Feasibility work differs across domains.

We cover:

- A. Banking & financial services
- B. SaaS and digital products
- C. Operations-heavy & regulated sectors

---

### A. Banking & Financial Services

Characteristics:

- legacy systems and complex integration landscapes,
- strict legal and regulatory requirements,
- multiple risk types and oversight functions.

Feasibility emphasis:

- aligning with:
  - core banking and risk architectures,
  - regulatory frameworks (KYC/AML, lending, payments),
  - security and data protection policies.

Common feasibility blockers:

- inflexible core systems,
- data residency or secrecy rules,
- incompatible vendor SLAs or controls.

---

### B. SaaS and Digital Products

Characteristics:

- more modern tech stacks,
- strong focus on:
  - scalability,
  - multi‑tenant architectures,
  - quick iteration.

Feasibility emphasis:

- platform and multi‑tenant considerations,
- integrations with other SaaS tools (APIs, webhooks),
- performance and reliability at scale.

Common feasibility questions:

- can we support:
  - large numbers of customers across regions,
  - customization without breaking multi‑tenant efficiency?

---

### C. Operations-Heavy & Regulated Sectors

Examples:

- telecoms,
- utilities,
- transport,
- healthcare.

Characteristics:

- large physical infrastructure and field operations,
- safety, reliability, and continuity critical,
- long asset lifecycles.

Feasibility emphasis:

- how digital changes interact with:
  - field operations,
  - existing infrastructure and processes,
  - safety protocols.

Common feasibility issues:

- complex organisational implications,
- multi‑year infrastructure changes,
- overlapping regulatory regimes.
