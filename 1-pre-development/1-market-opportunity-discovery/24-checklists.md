## 24 – Checklists for Market Opportunity Discovery

This file contains **compact checklists** you can use during discovery.  
They are derived from `02-objectives.md`, `03-workflow.md`, `20-best-practices.md`, and `21-common-mistakes.md`.

We include:

- A. Quick checklist: starting a discovery cycle
- B. Quick checklist: assessing an opportunity brief
- C. Quick checklist: before prioritization workshop
- D. Quick checklist: ending a discovery cycle

You can copy these into your own docs, tickets, or templates.

---

### A. Starting a Discovery Cycle – Checklist

**Scope & Objectives**

- [ ] Have we written a short **scope and objectives** document?
- [ ] Are we clear on:
  - [ ] which **segment(s)** we’re focusing on?
  - [ ] which **product/journey** we’re exploring?
  - [ ] the **key questions** we want to answer?

**Team & Stakeholders**

- [ ] Do we have a **named owner** (usually a PM)?
- [ ] Do we have core representation from:
  - [ ] Product / Strategy
  - [ ] UX/Research
  - [ ] Data / Analytics
  - [ ] Engineering
  - [ ] Risk/Compliance (if needed)
  - [ ] Operations / Support (if relevant)

**Plan & Timebox**

- [ ] Have we defined a **timebox** (start and end date)?
- [ ] Have we sketched major **milestones** (data review, synthesis, evaluation, readout)?

If most boxes are unchecked, **pause** and clarify before diving into data.

---

### B. Assessing an Opportunity Brief – Checklist

Use this when you or someone else has written an opportunity brief (see `10-documents.md`, `23-templates.md`).

**Basics**

- [ ] Clear **ID and title**.
- [ ] Short **summary** (1–3 sentences).

**Problem & Segment**

- [ ] Is the **target segment** clearly described?
- [ ] Is the **customer problem / job‑to‑be‑done** described in plain language?
- [ ] Do we know **when and where** this problem occurs?

**Evidence**

- [ ] Does the brief reference:
  - [ ] data/metrics,
  - [ ] customer feedback or interviews,
  - [ ] market/competitor info,
  - [ ] regulatory context (if relevant)?
- [ ] Are there **links or pointers** to underlying sources?

**Value & Fit**

- [ ] Does it explain **why this matters now**?
- [ ] Is there a **rough view of customer and business impact**?
- [ ] Is the **link to strategy / OKRs** explicit?

**Risks & Next Steps**

- [ ] Have **key assumptions and unknowns** been listed?
- [ ] Are key **risks** noted (product, execution, regulatory, organizational)?
- [ ] Are **recommended next steps** and **owners** specified?

If several of these are missing, the brief likely needs **more work** before evaluation.

---

### C. Before a Prioritization Workshop – Checklist

**Inputs Prepared**

- [ ] Do we have a **shortlist** of opportunities (e.g., 5–15) with briefs?
- [ ] Are briefs shared with participants **ahead of time**?

**Criteria & Process**

- [ ] Have we agreed on **evaluation criteria** (e.g., strategic fit, value, feasibility, risk)?
- [ ] Have we defined a **scoring scale** (e.g., 1–5) with simple explanations?
- [ ] Is it clear **who will participate** and **who decides**?

**Facilitation**

- [ ] Is there a **facilitator** to keep time and guide discussion?
- [ ] Do we have a simple way to **capture scores and notes** (sheet, board, tool)?

**Bias & Alignment**

- [ ] Have we invited **cross‑functional voices** (product, business, engineering, risk/compliance, operations)?
- [ ] Have we set expectations that:
  - [ ] scores are a **starting point**,  
  - [ ] disagreements are expected and helpful?

---

### D. Ending a Discovery Cycle – Checklist

**Outputs**

- [ ] Do we have:
  - [ ] documented **themes and insights**,
  - [ ] updated **opportunity backlog**,
  - [ ] **opportunity briefs** for top candidates,
  - [ ] evaluation / scoring outputs?

**Decisions**

- [ ] Have we clearly stated:
  - [ ] which opportunities **move forward now**,
  - [ ] which are **parked or discarded**,
  - [ ] and **why**?
- [ ] Are **owners** assigned for each top opportunity’s next phase?

**Communication**

- [ ] Have we shared a **readout** with:
  - [ ] leadership / decision‑makers,
  - [ ] relevant delivery teams,
  - [ ] supporting functions (risk, ops, etc.)?

**Learning & Improvement**

- [ ] Did we hold a short **retrospective** on the discovery process itself?
- [ ] Did we note:
  - [ ] what worked well,
  - [ ] what to change next time?

If you cannot check most of these at the end of a cycle, consider extending or revisiting certain steps before moving on.
