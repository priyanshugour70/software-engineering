## 10 – Documents & Artifacts in User & Market Research

This file explains the **key documents and artifacts** produced in this phase and how they are used.

We cover:

- A. Core artifacts
- B. Research plan
- C. Personas & segment profiles
- D. Journeys & service blueprints
- E. Insight reports & readouts

Templates live in `23-templates.md`.

---

### A. Core Artifacts

1. **Research Plan**
   - goals, questions, methods, participants, timeline.
2. **Raw Data & Notes**
   - transcripts, notes, tagged quotes, survey responses, analytics extracts.
3. **Models**
   - personas, segment profiles, journey maps, service blueprints.
4. **Insight Summary / Report**
   - synthesized findings and implications.
5. **Readout Decks / Sessions**
   - tailored communication for decisions and stakeholders.

---

### B. Research Plan

Purpose:

- align stakeholders on **why, what, and how** before starting.

Key sections:

- Context & problem statements.
- Decisions to support.
- Research questions & hypotheses.
- Methods and rationale.
- Participants & recruitment.
- Timeline and responsibilities.
- Ethical and compliance considerations.

Use:

- shared early with:
  - PMs,
  - design,
  - engineering,
  - business,
  - risk/compliance (where required).

---

### C. Personas & Segment Profiles

Purpose:

- capture **archetypes** of users based on evidence, not stereotypes.

Typical elements:

- segment name & short description,
- goals and jobs‑to‑be‑done,
- behaviors and patterns,
- constraints and environment,
- relationship to your product (how they use it today),
- key pains and needs.

Good personas are:

- tightly connected to:
  - data and research,
  - specific decisions (e.g., design focus, messaging, prioritization).

Avoid:

- demographic trivia that doesn’t affect decisions,
- fictional details unrelated to product use.

---

### D. User Journeys & Service Blueprints

**User journeys**:

- visual maps of:
  - stages,
  - steps,
  - touchpoints,
  - thoughts/feelings,
  - pain points.

**Service blueprints**:

- extend journeys by showing:
  - frontstage (user‑visible),
  - backstage (systems, processes),
  - support processes.

Use:

- to:
  - locate problems in context,
  - identify hand‑offs,
  - spot opportunities to redesign or automate.

---

### E. Insight Reports & Readouts

Insight report:

- narrative document including:
  - key findings,
  - evidence (quotes, screenshots, metrics),
  - implications,
  - recommended focus areas,
  - open questions.

Readout deck:

- shorter, presentation‑ready summary tailored to:
  - product teams,
  - leadership,
  - risk/ops,
depending on audience.

Best practices:

- start with:
  - 5–10 **headline insights**,
  - each with 1–3 pieces of evidence,
  - and 1–3 implications.
- link:
  - back to problem statements and decisions,
  - forward to roadmaps and requirements.
