## 24 – Checklists for User & Market Research

These compact checklists help ensure **research is focused, ethical, and actionable**.

We include:

- A. Before starting a study
- B. During collection
- C. Before readout and handover

---

### A. Before Starting a Study – Checklist

**Clarity & Focus**

- [ ] Problem statement(s) reviewed and understood.
- [ ] Decisions to support are explicitly listed.
- [ ] Research questions and hypotheses are written down.

**Plan**

- [ ] Research plan drafted (methods, participants, timeline).
- [ ] Methods chosen match the questions (qual vs quant, etc.).
- [ ] Participants and segments defined (who is in / out).

**Ethics & Compliance**

- [ ] Consent approach defined.
- [ ] Data handling/storage approach complies with policy.
- [ ] Legal/Compliance and InfoSec consulted where needed.

---

### B. During Data Collection – Checklist

**Execution**

- [ ] Sessions follow the agreed guide/script but allow for natural conversation.
- [ ] Moderators avoid leading questions.
- [ ] Observers understand their role (no interrupting participants).

**Coverage**

- [ ] We have participants from all **key segments/roles**.
- [ ] We have enough sessions to see clear patterns (or plan to continue).

**Data Handling**

- [ ] Notes are captured in a structured way.
- [ ] Recordings (if any) are stored in approved locations.
- [ ] Participant identities are not shared beyond what is necessary.

---

### C. Before Readout & Handover – Checklist

**Synthesis Quality**

- [ ] Key insights are clearly stated in plain language.
- [ ] Each insight is backed by:
  - [ ] quotes/observations **and/or**
  - [ ] metrics / survey results.
- [ ] Contradictory findings or outliers are noted, not hidden.

**Connection to Decisions**

- [ ] Insights are linked to:
  - [ ] specific problems,
  - [ ] strategic goals,
  - [ ] decisions they inform.
- [ ] Recommended changes or options are provided.

**Limitations**

- [ ] Sample size and composition are documented.
- [ ] Method constraints and biases are acknowledged.

**Integration**

- [ ] Problem Definition documents updated (if necessary).
- [ ] Requirements / PRDs incorporate key insights.
- [ ] Roadmaps or priorities adjusted where research justifies it.
- [ ] Artifacts stored in the central research space with clear titles and tags.
