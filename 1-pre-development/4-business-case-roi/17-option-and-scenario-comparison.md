## 17 – Option & Scenario Comparison

This file focuses on comparing **different ways to implement or scope** an initiative using Business Case & ROI thinking.

We cover:

- A. Option types
- B. Comparison dimensions
- C. Example comparison (MVP vs full rollout)

---

### A. Option Types

Common option dimensions:

- **Scope**
  - MVP vs full feature set,
  - single country vs multi‑country,
  - single segment vs all segments.
- **Timing**
  - big‑bang vs phased rollout,
  - now vs later (defer to next planning cycle).
- **Delivery model**
  - build vs buy vs partner,
  - centralized vs local execution.

Each option will:

- have different:
  - cost profiles,
  - benefit timings,
  - risk profiles.

---

### B. Comparison Dimensions

You can compare options along:

- **Financial metrics**
  - ROI, NPV, IRR, payback period.
- **Strategic fit**
  - alignment with top priorities,
  - enablers for future initiatives.
- **Risk**
  - delivery risk,
  - adoption risk,
  - regulatory risk.
- **Time to value**
  - how quickly meaningful benefits start.
- **Capacity & feasibility**
  - team availability,
  - technical constraints,
  - vendor readiness.

Tools:

- simple comparison tables,
- option‑by‑option financial models,
- qualitative scoring (e.g., 1–5 on risk, alignment).

---

### C. Example: MVP vs Full Digital SME Lending Rollout

**Option 1 – MVP in Country X for Existing SMEs Only**

- Scope:
  - digital application and decision for existing SME customers in one country.
- Cost:
  - lower initial build (smaller integration/scope),
  - lower run costs initially.
- Benefits:
  - smaller immediate volume,
  - earlier learning on adoption and risk.
- Risk:
  - lower delivery and adoption risk,
  - some strategic delay in global positioning.

**Option 2 – Full Rollout to Multiple Countries & Segments**

- Scope:
  - multiple countries,
  - existing and new‑to‑bank SMEs.
- Cost:
  - higher upfront engineering and integration,
  - more complex change and operations.
- Benefits:
  - much larger potential revenue and cost savings,
  - faster competitive catch‑up in key markets.
- Risk:
  - higher delivery risk (complex program),
  - adoption and localization challenges,
  - greater exposure if assumptions are wrong.

**Comparison Table (Conceptual)**

| Dimension       | Option 1 – MVP                      | Option 2 – Full Rollout                   |
|----------------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Initial cost   | Low–Medium                           | High                                      |
| Time to value  | Fast (pilot within 6–9 months)       | Slower (15–24 months to full value)      |
| 3–5 yr NPV     | Medium, with upside if scaled later  | High, but more sensitive to assumptions  |
| Delivery risk  | Medium–Low                           | High                                      |
| Strategic fit  | Good (learning & quick wins)         | Very high (if executed successfully)     |

Decision example:

- approve **Option 1** now as a phase 1,
- incorporate a **phase 2 plan** contingent on:
  - meeting defined adoption and performance metrics.
