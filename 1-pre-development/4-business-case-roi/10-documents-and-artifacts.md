## 10 – Documents & Artifacts in Business Case & ROI

This file explains the **key documents** produced in this phase and how they fit together.

We cover:

- A. Core artifacts
- B. Business case model (spreadsheet)
- C. Business case narrative document
- D. Executive/committee readout

Templates live in `23-templates.md`.

---

### A. Core Artifacts

1. **Driver & Assumption Sheet**
   - list of all key assumptions with sources.
2. **Financial Model**
   - multi‑year cash‑flow, ROI, NPV, IRR, scenarios.
3. **Business Case Document**
   - structured narrative with tables and charts.
4. **Readout Deck**
   - concise presentation for decision forums.
5. **Decision Record**
   - outcome, conditions, and targets.

---

### B. Business Case Model (Spreadsheet)

Purpose:

- turn assumptions and drivers into:
  - quantitative forecasts,
  - financial metrics.

Typical structure:

1. **Inputs**
   - adoption curves,
   - conversion uplift,
   - per‑unit revenue and cost,
   - build and run cost estimates,
   - discount rate.
2. **Calculations**
   - annual volumes and revenue,
   - cost savings,
   - incremental costs,
   - net cash flows.
3. **Outputs**
   - ROI, payback, NPV, IRR,
   - scenario and sensitivity tables.

Best practices:

- keep a **clean separation** between:
  - input cells,
  - formulas,
  - outputs.
- label and comment:
  - key input cells with:
    - sources,
    - date,
    - rationale.

### C. Business Case Narrative Document

Purpose:

- capture the **story, rationale, and evidence** in a way that non‑financial stakeholders can understand.

Sections (suggested):

1. **Executive Summary**
   - short statement of:
     - what is proposed,
     - why now,
     - key financials,
     - recommendation.
2. **Context & Strategic Fit**
   - link to:
     - problem definition,
     - research,
     - strategic objectives.
3. **Solution Overview & Scope**
   - high‑level description of what is being funded,
   - in/out of scope.
4. **Benefits**
   - detailed description and tables:
     - revenue, cost, risk, intangibles.
5. **Costs & Investments**
   - one‑time and ongoing,
   - timelines.
6. **Financial Analysis**
   - summary of:
     - ROI, NPV, IRR, payback,
     - scenarios and sensitivities.
7. **Risks & Dependencies**
   - key risks,
   - mitigations,
   - dependencies and their status.
8. **Recommendation**
   - proposed decision and rationale,
   - potential alternatives considered.

---

### D. Executive / Committee Readout

Purpose:

- present the case in a **short, digestible** format for senior decision‑makers.

Content:

- 1–2 slides:
  - executive summary and strategic fit.
- 2–3 slides:
  - benefits and costs,
  - key metrics (ROI, NPV, payback),
  - base & downside scenario.
- 1–2 slides:
  - key risks,
  - dependencies,
  - mitigation strategies.
- 1 slide:
  - explicit **ask** (funding, phasing, decision),
  - and conditions (if any).

Best practices:

- focus on:
  - clarity and trade‑offs,
  - not exhaustive detail.
- have:
  - the full narrative and model ready as backup.
