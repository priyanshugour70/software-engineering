## 22 – Governance & Approval Processes

This file explains how Business Case & ROI work fits into **formal governance** in enterprises and banks.

We cover:

- A. Typical approval layers
- B. Stage‑gate patterns
- C. Practical tips for navigating governance

---

### A. Typical Approval Layers

In many organizations (especially banks), investment decisions involve:

- **Domain or Segment Committees**
  - e.g., SME Steering Committee, Retail Product Council.
- **Cross-Functional Boards**
  - technology steering groups,
  - architecture boards.
- **Portfolio / Investment Committees**
  - decide major funding allocations across initiatives.
- **Executive Committees / Board**
  - approve very large or strategic programs.

Business cases:

- may be reviewed at multiple levels,
- with:
  - increasing scrutiny,
  - broader portfolio context.

---

### B. Stage-Gate Patterns

Common stages:

1. **Idea / Concept Gate**
   - high‑level opportunity,
   - strategic alignment,
   - rough value potential.
   - Output:
     - permission to explore further.

2. **Discovery / Validation Gate**
   - problem definition,
   - user & market research,
   - early solution options.
   - Output:
     - go/no‑go for detailed business case and solution design.

3. **Investment Gate**
   - full business case and ROI,
   - risk and dependency assessment,
   - portfolio comparison.
   - Output:
     - funding approval or rejection,
     - priorities and phasing.

4. **Delivery / Release Gates**
   - stage funding releases,
   - architecture/security reviews,
   - readiness checks.

5. **Post-Implementation Review**
   - check actuals vs business case,
   - feed learnings into future cases.

### C. Practical Tips for Navigating Governance

1. **Know the Calendar and Cycles**
   - planning and funding cycles (annual/quarterly),
   - committee schedules.

2. **Engage Early with Key Stakeholders**
   - not just at formal gate meetings:
     - pre‑socialize:
       - problem,
       - research,
       - draft case,
     with:
       - business leaders,
       - finance,
       - strategy,
       - risk.

3. **Tailor Communication**
   - committees care about:
     - different things:
       - architecture board:
         - technical feasibility and risk,
       - investment committee:
         - financial return and strategic fit,
       - risk committee:
         - regulatory and risk impact.

4. **Bring Options, Not Just One Proposal**
   - present:
     - MVP vs full scope,
     - alternative timings,
     - or build vs buy.

5. **Document Decisions and Conditions**
   - record:
     - what was approved,
     - conditions or metrics required,
     - who owns follow‑ups.

6. **Close the Loop with Post-Launch Evaluations**
   - share:
     - actual performance vs business case,
     - key learnings,
   with:
     - the same governance bodies.
