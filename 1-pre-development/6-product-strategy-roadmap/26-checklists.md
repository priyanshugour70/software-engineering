## 26 – Checklists for Product Strategy & Roadmapping

This file provides **short, practical checklists** derived from this whole module.

Use them when:

- preparing a strategy refresh,
- building or updating a roadmap,
- running reviews or alignment sessions.

---

### 1. Strategy Quality Checklist

Before you say “our product strategy is ready,” check:

- [ ] **Vision**
  - [ ] states who we serve,
  - [ ] states what we enable them to do,
  - [ ] states why it matters (value),
  - [ ] is understandable by a new joiner or non‑expert.
- [ ] **Diagnosis**
  - [ ] clearly explains current situation and problems,
  - [ ] uses data and insights (not only opinions).
- [ ] **Strategic Pillars / Themes**
  - [ ] 3–7 themes, each with a clear purpose,
  - [ ] directly connected to diagnosis and vision.
- [ ] **Scope**
  - [ ] states where we will play (segments, geos, products, channels),
  - [ ] states where we will **not** play (for this horizon).
- [ ] **How We Will Win**
  - [ ] describes differentiators (experience, data, risk, ecosystem, etc.),
  - [ ] links differentiators to customer needs and competitors.
- [ ] **Key Bets & Capabilities**
  - [ ] lists a small number of major bets,
  - [ ] clarifies enabling capabilities needed.

If many boxes are unchecked, revisit `04-vision-and-product-strategy.md`.

---

### 2. Themes & Goals Checklist

- [ ] We have:
  - [ ] 3–7 strategic themes,
  - [ ] each with a short description.
- [ ] For each theme:
  - [ ] at least one objective is defined,
  - [ ] 2–5 key results or metrics are defined,
  - [ ] there is a clear owner (person/role).
- [ ] Themes and goals:
  - [ ] align with company/segment strategy,
  - [ ] are visible in roadmap and backlog tooling,
  - [ ] are known by key stakeholders.

If gaps exist, see `05-strategic-themes-goals-and-okrs.md`.

---

### 3. Initiative & Roadmap Checklist

For your initiative catalogue and roadmap:

- [ ] Initiatives and epics:
  - [ ] are documented in one catalogue (not scattered),
  - [ ] each has a problem statement or outcome description,
  - [ ] are linked to at least one theme and objective.
- [ ] Prioritization:
  - [ ] uses a consistent framework (e.g., RICE, WSJF),
  - [ ] considers value, effort, risk, and urgency,
  - [ ] is revisited at least quarterly.
- [ ] Roadmap:
  - [ ] uses multi‑horizon or now/next/later view,
  - [ ] distinguishes committed vs tentative items,
  - [ ] includes non‑feature work (platform, risk, ops).

For visuals and structure, see `06-prioritization-frameworks.md` and `07-roadmap-levels-and-views.md`.

---

### 4. Capacity & Dependency Checklist

- [ ] Capacity:
  - [ ] we know how many teams/squads we have,
  - [ ] we have a sense of throughput per team (e.g., epics/quarter),
  - [ ] our roadmap does **not** assume impossible capacity.
- [ ] Allocation:
  - [ ] we have explicit percentages or ranges for:
    - growth/features,
    - platform/tech,
    - risk/compliance,
    - operations/analytics.
- [ ] Dependencies:
  - [ ] major cross‑team dependencies are documented,
  - [ ] enabling platform/risk work is sequenced properly,
  - [ ] we regularly review dependency risks.

See `09-capacity-planning-and-funding-models.md` and `10-dependencies-and-sequencing.md` for more detail.

---

### 5. Governance & Alignment Checklist

- [ ] Forums:
  - [ ] we have at least one regular roadmap/portfolio review (e.g., quarterly),
  - [ ] cross‑team dependency/risk syncs happen regularly,
  - [ ] risk/compliance governance is integrated where needed.
- [ ] Decisions:
  - [ ] are documented (who decided, why),
  - [ ] result in updated artifacts (roadmaps, docs),
  - [ ] are communicated to relevant stakeholders.
- [ ] Cadence:
  - [ ] strategy refresh at least annually,
  - [ ] roadmap adjustments at least quarterly,
  - [ ] team‑level planning and retros on regular cycles.

See `24-governance-alignment-and-cadence.md` for structures and examples.

---

### 6. Communication & Documentation Checklist

- [ ] We have:
  - [ ] a clearly labeled strategy doc,
  - [ ] a themes & goals doc,
  - [ ] roadmap views for different audiences,
  - [ ] an initiative/epic catalogue.
- [ ] Docs:
  - [ ] are easy to find (e.g., dedicated space in `Confluence`/`Notion`),
  - [ ] have owners,
  - [ ] are updated after major changes.
- [ ] Communication:
  - [ ] changes to strategy or roadmap are explained:
    - what changed,
    - why,
    - what the impact is.

See `12-documents-and-artifacts.md` and `25-templates.md` for structures and templates.

---

### 7. Personal Skill Growth Checklist (for PMs and Leaders)

- [ ] Strategic thinking:
  - [ ] I can articulate vision, themes, and outcomes clearly.
- [ ] Customer & market understanding:
  - [ ] I know key user segments and their main jobs/pains.
- [ ] Analytical & financial:
  - [ ] I can interpret basic metrics and ROI analyses.
- [ ] Technical & systems:
  - [ ] I understand main systems, data flows, and key dependencies.
- [ ] Collaboration & facilitation:
  - [ ] I can run aligned roadmap sessions with multiple stakeholders.
- [ ] Governance & risk:
  - [ ] I understand how risk/compliance impacts our roadmap.

If you see gaps, `14-skills.md` offers learning directions.
