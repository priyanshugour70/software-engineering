## 11 – Tools for Feasibility Studies

This file lists practical tools that support Feasibility work.

We group them into:

- A. Architecture & design tools
- B. Process & mapping tools
- C. Risk & tracking tools
- D. Documentation & collaboration

---

### A. Architecture & Design Tools

- **Diagramming tools**
  - e.g., Draw.io, Lucidchart, PlantUML, architecture plugins in IDEs.
  - Use for:
    - system/context diagrams,
    - data flow diagrams,
    - interface/integration maps.

- **Architecture repositories**
  - e.g., internal EA tools, wiki spaces.
  - Use for:
    - storing reference architectures,
    - documenting patterns and standards.

---

### B. Process & Mapping Tools

- **Miro / FigJam / Whiteboards**
  - for:
    - mapping current and to‑be processes,
    - mapping responsibilities and handoffs.

- **BPM / Process modeling tools**
  - e.g., Signavio, Visio.
  - for:
    - more formal process models,
    - linking to operational procedures.

---

### C. Risk & Tracking Tools

- **Risk registers / GRC tools**
  - internal GRC (Governance, Risk, Compliance) platforms,
  - or simple spreadsheets.
  - Use for:
    - tracking risks, constraints, mitigations, owners.

- **Issue & dependency tracking**
  - Jira, Azure DevOps, etc.
  - Use for:
    - logging feasibility issues,
    - tracking enabling tasks.

---

### D. Documentation & Collaboration

- **Confluence / Notion / SharePoint**
  - Use for:
    - feasibility reports,
    - meeting notes,
    - decision records.

- **Meeting & workshop tools**
  - video conferencing + shared boards/docs.
  - Use for:
    - cross‑functional feasibility workshops,
    - architecture/risk/legal discussions.
