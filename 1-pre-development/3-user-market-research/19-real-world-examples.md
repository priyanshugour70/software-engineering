## 19 – Real-World Style Examples (Micro-Scenarios)

These short scenarios show **specific research moves** in practical contexts.

We cover:

- A. Tightening research questions
- B. Using analytics to pick interview participants
- C. Updating problem definitions after research

---

### A. Tightening Research Questions

Initial vague goal:

> “We want to understand why SMEs don’t use our reports.”

Refinement using `04-research-questions-and-hypotheses.md`:

1. Identify decisions:
   - “Should we invest in improving current reports, building new ones, or integrating with external tools?”
2. Translate into questions:
   - “In their day‑to‑day work, how do SMEs currently monitor financial performance?”
   - “What do they try to achieve when they open our reports?”
   - “Why do some SMEs always export data instead of using in‑portal views?”
3. Hypotheses:
   - “We believe that our report structure doesn’t match how SMEs think about their business (e.g., cash‑flow vs profit).”

Impact:

- Interviews and analytics now focus on:
  - specific behaviors,
  - concrete decisions.

---

### B. Using Analytics to Pick Interview Participants

Problem:

> “Abandonment in online onboarding is high.”

Analytics:

- funnel shows:
  - biggest drop at Step 3 (documents),
  - mobile users have higher drop‑off.

Recruitment strategy:

- invite:
  - SMEs who:
    - started onboarding and dropped at Step 3,
    - started on mobile vs desktop.

Benefits:

- ensures:
  - interviewees **experienced the phenomenon** you’re investigating,
  - you can compare:
    - mobile vs desktop patterns.

Outcome:

- more targeted and insightful interviews,
- faster pattern recognition.

---

### C. Updating Problem Definitions After Research

Initial problem statement:

> “SMEs don’t upload documents because the process is too long.”

Research findings:

- interviews show:
  - main issues are:
    - unclear requirements,
    - lack of security reassurance,
    - feeling unprepared.
- time in the flow itself:
  - less of a complaint than expected.

Updated problem statement:

> “SMEs often abandon online onboarding at the document upload step because they are unsure which documents are required, don’t trust uploading sensitive files without clearer security assurances, and frequently realize they are not ready with all documents, leading them to postpone or switch to branch.”

Impact:

- design team focuses:
  - not only on speed,
  - but on:
    - clarity,
    - trust,
    - preparation support.
