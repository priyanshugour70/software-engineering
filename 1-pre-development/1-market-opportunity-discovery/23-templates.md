## 23 – Templates for Market Opportunity Discovery

This file provides **ready‑to‑use markdown templates** you can copy into your own docs or tools.  
They are designed to be **simple and beginner‑friendly**, but still aligned with the rest of this handbook.

We include templates for:

- A. Discovery scope & plan
- B. Opportunity brief
- C. Opportunity backlog (table)
- D. Evaluation scoring sheet
- E. Discovery readout

You can adapt names and sections to your organization’s style.

---

### A. Discovery Scope & Plan Template

```markdown
# Discovery Scope & Plan – <Domain / Segment / Product>

## 1. Context
- Business context:
  - <Why are we looking at this area now?>
- Strategic goals / OKRs related:
  - <List relevant company/product objectives>

## 2. Key Questions
- Q1: <Main question we want to answer>
- Q2: <Secondary question>
- Q3: <Optional>

## 3. Scope
- Segments:
  - <e.g., SMEs with 0.5–5M revenue in retail sector>
- Geography:
  - <Countries or regions>
- Product / Journey focus:
  - <e.g., SME onboarding, lending, payments>

## 4. Timebox and Milestones
- Start date:
- End date:
- Key milestones:
  - Week 1–2: <e.g., data review & interviews>
  - Week 3–4: <e.g., synthesis & opportunity briefs>
  - Week 5–6: <e.g., evaluation & readout>

## 5. Team & Stakeholders
- Core team:
  - Product:
  - Strategy/Business:
  - UX/Research:
  - Data:
  - Engineering:
  - Risk/Compliance:
  - Operations/Support:
- Key stakeholders to consult:
  - <Names / roles>

## 6. Deliverables
- <Number> opportunity briefs
- Updated opportunity backlog
- Evaluation / prioritization outputs
- Final readout to <committee / leadership group>
```

### B. Opportunity Brief Template

```markdown
# Opportunity Brief – <ID> <Short Title>

## 1. Summary
<2–3 sentence high‑level description of the opportunity.>

## 2. Target Segment
- Who:
  - <Customer/segment description>
- Where:
  - <Geography / channel if relevant>

## 3. Customer Problem / Job-To-Be-Done
- When <situation>…
- <Segment> wants to <job / goal>…
- So they can <desired outcome>.

Describe current pains:
- <Pain 1>
- <Pain 2>
- <Pain 3>

## 4. Current Journey & Alternatives
- How do customers solve this today?
- Which tools / competitors / manual processes do they use?

## 5. Opportunity Description
- What is the **space** where we can create value?
- Avoid specific features; focus on **what would be better for the customer**.

## 6. Value & Impact
### For Customers
- <Value point 1>
- <Value point 2>

### For Business
- Potential revenue or growth impact:
- Potential cost reduction:
- Potential risk reduction:

## 7. Strategic Fit
- How does this support company / product strategy or OKRs?
- Which strategic themes does it align with?

## 8. Evidence & Signals
Summarize key inputs and link to sources:
- Analytics:
  - <Metric + link/screenshot>
- Customer feedback:
  - <Quotes / themes + link to notes>
- Market / competitors:
  - <Key findings + links>
- Regulation:
  - <Relevant rules / guidance + links>

## 9. Risks, Unknowns, Assumptions
- Assumptions:
  - <Assumption 1>
  - <Assumption 2>
- Key unknowns:
  - <Unknown 1>
  - <Unknown 2>
- Risks:
  - Product/market:
  - Execution:
  - Regulatory/compliance:
  - Organizational:

## 10. Initial Evaluation Snapshot (Optional)
- Strategic fit: <1–5>
- Customer value: <1–5>
- Business impact: <1–5>
- Feasibility: <1–5>
- Time sensitivity: <1–5>
- Risk level: <1–5> (or described textually)

## 11. Recommended Next Steps
- Move to:
  - <Problem Definition / Research / Feasibility / etc.>
- Next activities:
  - <e.g., 5 customer interviews, deeper data pull, risk workshop>
- Proposed owner(s):
  - <Name / team>
```

### D. Evaluation Scoring Sheet Template

```markdown
# Opportunity Evaluation – <Date / Cycle Name>

## Criteria and Scale
- 1 = Very low / poor
- 2 = Low
- 3 = Medium
- 4 = High
- 5 = Very high / excellent

Criteria:
- C1: Strategic fit
- C2: Customer value
- C3: Business impact
- C4: Feasibility (high-level)
- C5: Time sensitivity / urgency
- C6: Risk (you may treat this as “lower is better” or invert)

## Scores

| ID     | Title                    | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 | C6 | Notes (key reasons / assumptions) |
|--------|--------------------------|----|----|----|----|----|----|------------------------------------|
| MOD-001| Digital SME Credit Line  | 5  | 5  | 5  | 3  | 4  | 3  | High strategic priority, strong demand, medium feasibility. |
| MOD-002| Cash-Flow Forecasting    | 4  | 4  | 4  | 4  | 3  | 3  | Complementary to MOD-001, good differentiation. |
| MOD-003| Payment Status Transparency | 3| 3  | 2  | 5  | 4  | 2  | Quick win, smaller standalone impact. |

## Discussion Summary
- Main disagreements and how they were resolved:
  - <Notes>
- Final prioritized shortlist:
  - 1) MOD-001
  - 2) MOD-002
  - 3) MOD-003
```

### E. Discovery Readout Template

```markdown
# Discovery Readout – <Domain / Segment> – <Date>

## 1. Context & Objectives
- Why we ran this discovery:
  - <Short explanation>
- Scope:
  - <Segments, products, geographies>
- Timebox:
  - <Dates>

## 2. Methods & Inputs
- Data sources:
  - <Analytics dashboards, BI, reports, etc.>
- Research activities:
  - <# of interviews, workshops, surveys>
- Other inputs:
  - <Competitor analysis, regulatory review, etc.>

## 3. Key Themes & Insights
- Theme 1: <Name>
  - <Short description + supporting evidence>
- Theme 2: <Name>
  - <...>
- Theme 3: <Name>
  - <...>

## 4. Opportunity Shortlist
- MOD-001 – <Title>
  - Segment:
  - Problem:
  - Why now:
  - Link: [Opportunity Brief](./MOD-001.md)
- MOD-002 – <Title>
  - ...

## 5. Evaluation Summary
- Criteria used:
  - <Strategic fit, customer value, etc.>
- Visuals:
  - <Insert 2x2 matrix or summary table if in slide format>
- Key conclusions:
  - <Short bullets>

## 6. Recommendations
- Move forward now:
  - MOD-<ID> – <Title> – next phase: <e.g., Problem Definition>
- Explore later / monitor:
  - MOD-<ID> – <Title>
- Park / discard:
  - MOD-<ID> – <Title> – reason: <short explanation>

## 7. Next Steps & Owners
- Owners:
  - <Name> (Product)
  - <Name> (Engineering)
  - <Name> (Risk/Compliance)
- Immediate actions (next 4–8 weeks):
  - <Action 1>
  - <Action 2>
  - <Action 3>
```
