## 11 – Roles and Positions in Business Case & ROI

This file explains **who participates** in building and approving business cases.

We cover:

- A. Core accountable roles
- B. Supporting roles
- C. Example setups
- D. RACI-style overview

---

### A. Core Accountable Roles

#### 1. Business Owner / P&L Owner

Responsibilities:

- Owns **commercial outcomes** for the initiative.
- Ensures:
  - business case aligns with strategy and P&L targets.
- Accountable for:
  - recommending whether to proceed,
  - benefit realization post‑launch.

---

#### 2. Product Manager / Product Owner

Responsibilities:

- Connects:
  - problem and research,
  - solution options,
  - and business value.
- Co‑creates:
  - drivers and assumptions,
  - narrative and recommendations.

Acts as:

- integrator between:
  - business,
  - tech,
  - finance,
  - risk.

---

#### 3. Finance Partner

Responsibilities:

- Validates:
  - financial models,
  - assumptions formats,
  - metrics.
- Ensures:
  - alignment with finance policies,
  - correct treatment of CapEx/OpEx,
  - appropriate discount rates.

Often:

- co‑presents to committees,
- acts as a **credibility anchor** for numbers.

---

### B. Supporting Roles

#### 4. Data / BI Analyst

Responsibilities:

- Provide:
  - baselines,
  - behavioral metrics,
  - cost‑to‑serve figures.
- Support:
  - scenario analysis,
  - sensitivity checks.

---

#### 5. Engineering Manager / Architect

Responsibilities:

- Estimate:
  - build effort and complexity,
  - technical risk,
  - infrastructure implications.
- Identify:
  - dependencies,
  - reusable components.

---

#### 6. Operations / Service Owners

Responsibilities:

- Estimate:
  - operational impact,
  - potential cost savings,
  - staffing changes.
- Surface:
  - process constraints,
  - change management needs.

---

#### 7. Risk & Compliance

Responsibilities:

- Identify:
  - regulatory implications,
  - risk reduction benefits.
- Flag:
  - risk of non‑compliance,
  - required controls and monitoring.

---

#### 8. Strategy / Portfolio Management

Responsibilities:

- Ensure:
  - alignment with long‑term strategy.
- Compare:
  - cases across the portfolio.
- Prepare:
  - views for investment committees.

---

### C. Example Setups

#### 1. Line-of-Business Initiative (Banking)

- Business Owner (Head of SME),
- PM (SME lending),
- Finance Partner (assigned to SME),
- Data Analyst (shared),
- Eng Manager (core lending platform),
- Risk/Compliance rep,
- Strategy/Portfolio rep.

They collaborate to:

- produce the case,
- take it to the bank’s Investment Committee.

---

#### 2. Cross-Cutting Platform Investment

- Sponsor: CTO / Head of Technology,
- PM: Platform/Product lead,
- Finance Partner: Technology finance,
- Strategy: Enterprise architecture & portfolio.

Focuses:

- more on:
  - cost and risk,
  - enablement of future benefits.

---

### D. RACI-Style Overview (Simplified)

| Activity                              | Business Owner | PM  | Finance | Data/BI | Eng Mgr | Ops | Risk/Comp | Strategy |
|---------------------------------------|----------------|-----|---------|---------|--------|-----|-----------|----------|
| Define scope & strategic context      | A              | R   | C       | C       | C      | C   | C         | C        |
| Identify value & cost drivers         | A              | R   | C       | C       | C      | C   | C         | C        |
| Build benefit & cost estimates        | C              | R   | C       | R       | C      | C   | C         | C        |
| Build & validate financial model      | C              | R   | A       | R       | C      | C   | C         | C        |
| Document business case & recommendation| A             | R   | C       | C       | C      | C   | C         | C        |
| Review in committees                  | A              | R   | R       | C       | C      | C   | C         | C        |

R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted.
